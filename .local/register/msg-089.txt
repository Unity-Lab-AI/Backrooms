Bringing a gate up is work (0.8.9-dev)

Opening a laboratory connection is no longer a button. The assigned
operator brings the gate up at the console over time, at their own
working speed, with the progress shown on the console.

Every entry point routes through the one ramp: dialling a remembered
address, opening a session from the operations window, and opening
after registering. There is exactly one way a laboratory gate opens,
so the ramp cannot be skipped by choosing a different button.

The ramp climbs only while the gate is genuinely held, by the same
conditions a live opening is held by, and bleeds otherwise. At zero it
lapses with the address kept. Required work falls with every previous
connection to the same address, down to a floor, which turns the gate
address book into its learned routes and gives pinning something real
to protect. A finished ramp waits fully charged rather than being
discarded when the battery is momentarily short.

Decay was first written as a flat rate per tick with a comment claiming
it was slower than progress. It was not: at low Intellectual a
technician would have lost charge faster than they could build it,
making a slow operator's gate impossible rather than slow. The offline
proof rejected it on that assertion. Decay is now a fraction of the
observed climb rate, so the guarantee holds for every operator by
construction rather than by luck.

A designated gate is a blue door with a blue glow around it. This
needed no new component and no new patch operation: ThingWithComps
honours ThingComp.ForceColor(), and the gate component is already on
the door, so every other door in the game is untouched. A colour the
player painted on still wins. The aura already existed but only
appeared while open; it is now the gate's permanent identity.

Natural gates are unchanged and again needed no new guard.
