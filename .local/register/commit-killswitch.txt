A cutoff you can throw (0.7.0-dev)

An optional emergency cutoff for a laboratory gate: a Core power switch on the
gate's own circuit that somebody can throw to shut an open gate at once.

What already happened, stated before what is new, because it changes what needed
building. Cutting power to an open gate already closed it: TickGate tests
HasPowerAndHeadroom every tick and calls EnterEmergency("RR_Gate_PowerLost"),
which starts the bounded emergency-return window. And Core's own
Building_PowerSwitch stops transmitting when open, so a switch wired upstream
already cut the supply and already closed the gate. The physics was most of the
way there.

What was missing was everything that turns it from an accident into a control.
The gate had no idea which switch was its own, so it could not say so. Nothing
verified the switch was actually on the gate's circuit, so a player could build
one, believe it was the cutoff, and find out otherwise in the one moment it
mattered. And a deliberate shutdown and a snapped conduit produced the identical
message.

The design is that the wiring is real rather than cosmetic. A switch may only be
bound while it is closed and while it and the gate sit on the same power net.
That single condition separates a real kill switch from a decoration: sharing a
net while closed means opening it necessarily severs the gate from its supply,
using Core's own power graph rather than simulating anything. Requiring it
closed at bind time is why the check can be that simple, because an open switch
has already split the net and there would be nothing to compare. Matched by
capability rather than by name, so a modded switch works unnamed. One switch to
one gate, because two gates sharing a cutoff would mean one flick closed both.

The tick checks the cutoff before the generic power test, and that ordering is
the point: a thrown switch would cut the supply a tick later anyway and the
cause recorded would be "power lost", indistinguishable from a broken wire.

The emergency-return window is deliberately kept. It is the entire reason the
gate reserves its own watt-days, and removing it would mean one flick
permanently strands everybody on the far side. "Instantly closes" is honoured as
the opening ending the moment the switch is thrown, while those already through
keep the bounded chance to come back that every other emergency gives them.

One consequence worth stating out loud because it is a feature: flicking is
ordinary work through Core's Flick designation, and cross-gate BasicWorker
support landed in 0.6.7-dev, so somebody at home can be ordered to throw the
cutoff while a team is still inside. That is exactly the scenario it exists for.

Optional, as a separate call rather than a fourth argument on the existing
binding, so every saved gate is untouched and none needs rebinding. The link
re-derives from its saved reference on every read, so a deconstructed or
relocated switch stops being the cutoff with nothing having to clear it.

The owner's follow-up direction in the same session was checked against shipped
values rather than assumed, and three of four already matched exactly: power
loss already closes an open gate every tick; the first opening is already
exactly 30 real minutes, since portalBaseWindowTicks is 108000 and 108000 over
60 ticks per second is 1800 seconds; it already only increases from there at
three times per earned tier; and tiers already come from completed research
rather than spendable insight. The genuinely new part is equipment maintenance,
which does not exist anywhere in the gate today, and a player-facing how-to.
Both are recorded verbatim in TODO.md and scoped to their own checkpoints rather
than folded into this one.
