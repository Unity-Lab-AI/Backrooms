The gate assembly needs looking after (0.7.1-dev)

Gate servicing, modelled on how Questionable Ethics Enhanced runs its cloning
and organ vats, which is what the owner was pointing at.

A misreading of mine, corrected by the owner and worth recording. I read
"questionable ethics" as flavour and went as far as asking which way to take the
ethics angle, framing it as a content-policy question. It is a mod name, profile
row 182, and the owner meant a concrete mechanic with numbers behind it. The
wrong reading would have produced atmospheric text and no system. The register
recovered it in one query: row 182's review carried the package id and the local
install path, which led to the mod's own defs and its own description.

Their model, from their own shipped description: "Requires regular maintenance
by a skilled scientist and doctor. A sterile room will significantly decrease
the maintenance required. If the vat loses power, it will rapidly lose
maintenance." Plus a dedicated low-priority maintenance giver and a job whose
report string is "monitoring and adjusting" rather than repairing.

Three ideas, all better than a service timer: a condition that decays
continuously; the room modulating the decay; and power loss degrading it fast.
The owner's "cool down dead zone" falls out of that rather than being bolted on
-- a well-kept room decays so slowly nobody is called for a long stretch, a
filthy one calls somebody constantly, so the player controls the dead zone by
looking after the place.

Built as a condition in ticks, ten days at full, wearing at half rate in a
sterile room, triple in a filthy one, eight times without power and three times
while a connection is held open, clamped at both ends. Cleanliness is
RoomStatDefOf.Cleanliness, Core's own number.

The dead zone is also a hard threshold, and that part is load-bearing. Offering
the work whenever condition is below full would be the growth-vat-one-nutrition-
short trap and would have a pawn topping the gate up continuously, which is
exactly what the direction rules out. Offered only below a quarter, restores
full in one visit. ServiceWanted is false across the whole band, so the giver's
scan finds nothing and costs one boolean per console for most of a game.

It plugs into what already exists rather than sitting beside it. Cleanliness is
kept by the cleaning family, which already crosses a gate, so a chamber either
side of a portal is kept by the same work. Power ties to the kill switch built
the checkpoint before, which now costs more than closing the gate. Skill reuses
the Research work type calibration already uses, so no new work type is added.

Lapsing stops the next opening and never closes one already running: ending an
opening for a bookkeeping reason would strand whoever is on the far side, and
the return window is for real emergencies. Cost is work time by a skilled
colonist and nothing else, because the owner's ceiling is explicit -- not "crazy
amounts" -- and a resource cost on a recurring chore is how maintenance becomes
a tax.

Nothing of that mod is copied, referenced or depended on. Its defs and assembly
are untouched and this works with it absent. The idea was read from its public
description exactly as every profile row is read.

Saved as one integer defaulting to minus one, which reads as full, so every gate
in a 0.7.0-dev save loads in full condition rather than lapsed.

NOW.md is rewritten as a compaction handoff: state, the standing instruction,
what exists, what is left in order, thirty-one invariants, the standing method,
the reading order, the checkpoint ritual and the gotchas.
