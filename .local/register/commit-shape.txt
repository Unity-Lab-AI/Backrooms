The shape of the place, and hallways (0.8.6-dev)

The room-shape half of the echo direction, outstanding since 0.8.1-dev and the
last named loose end. Deep coordinates are no longer built to a tidy grid: rooms
stretch, shrink and go wrong, they go wronger the further in they sit, some take
the proportions of rooms the branch actually built, and a good number are
deliberate hallways.

The fingerprint problem was the whole difficulty. Layout planning is re-run to
verify a saved graph against its fingerprint, so a planner that consulted the
colony's current rooms would replan a coordinate differently the moment the
player built an extension -- and that coordinate would then fail its own
fingerprint check and refuse to generate. So echoed dimensions are captured once,
at discovery, and saved on the coordinate. Never read live. That is also the
better fiction: the place copied what it saw when it opened, not what you have
built since.

Existing coordinates are untouched by design. roomLibraryVersion already existed
for exactly this and already feeds the fingerprint, so new coordinates are
version two and everything discovered before plans byte-identically to how it
always did.

Shallow stays regular, deliberately. Depth one does not derange at all, because
the yellow rooms read as a place precisely because they are monotonous and
regular, and deranging them would throw away the image the whole setting rests
on. The wrongness is something the player travels toward.

Hallways are made deliberately rather than hoped for. A corridor is one of the
two shapes the setting is actually built on, and leaving it to a symmetric
stretch roll would have produced one rarely and by accident. It gets its own
branch: long and narrow on one axis, which the room dresser then fills as a
corridor rather than as a hall.

Deranged must not collapse into broken. Every dimension is clamped to eight
through seventeen, because rooms sit nineteen cells apart on the planning grid
and anything wider would overlap its neighbour -- at which point the candidate
validator would reject every layout, the planner would fall back to the plain
one, and the feature would silently become a no-op while appearing to work. That
was not left to trust: the placement formula was replicated offline and every
clamped width and height combination from eight to seventeen checked for overlap
and for map bounds, with zero violations and extents of one to fifty-five inside
a sixty by sixty map. The check was re-run after the hallway shapes were added.

Two parts of the direction were already met and are said so rather than rebuilt.
Furnishing rooms with their proper equipment, and items, are both already covered
by the archetype library from 0.7.9-dev: fourteen archetypes filling rooms by
capability, including item slots drawn from thing categories, so a workshop gets
benches and material and a ward gets beds and medicine. The furnishing half was
not outstanding; only the mapping is.
