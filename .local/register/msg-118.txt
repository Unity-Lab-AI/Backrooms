The storyteller finally knows this mod exists (0.11.8-dev)

The flavour half of the owner's decision at the fork: "Both - guaranteed
floor, storyteller flavour". The floor shipped in 0.11.7; this is the other
half.

Until this checkpoint the mod shipped ZERO IncidentDefs. Every event fired
from its own GameComponent tick, so whichever storyteller the player chose had
never heard of the mod and never paced a single thing it did. A mod that runs
beside the game's event economy instead of inside it always reads as bolted
on, however good the content is.

Two incidents, both Core-only, both Misc, neither able to hurt a pawn, destroy
a thing or block a route:

- RR_Incident_ThresholdBleed. The space comes out the near side: the lights in
  the room a designated gate stands in go out, or the room drops eight degrees,
  or dirt appears that was not there. Scoped to the gate's room, never the
  colony, which is what makes the rule learnable. Requires the branch to have
  visited a coordinate, because the space cannot follow a crew home from
  somewhere the crew has never been.
- RR_Incident_CorporationDelivery. A crate nobody ordered, gated on corporation
  contact. Greed is the mechanism for the generosity, not a contradiction of
  it. Deliberately modest at 40% of the clean-up team's crate.

No StorytellerDef, and the reason is not effort. It is an exclusive slot the
player would have to give up Cassandra or Randy for, it needs portrait art the
no-new-art rule forbids, and there is no intelligence in one to borrow: a
StorytellerComp rolls a mean-time-between against wealth and population. The
part that behaves like a director is IncidentWorker.CanFireNowSub, which is
ours without owning anything. proof-incidents.py now asserts the never.

AnomalyEventService was re-scoped from CoordinateRecord to a plain cell list so
the bleed runs the SAME four effect bodies a coordinate runs. What would drift
out of a second copy are the four safety promises in invariant 28.

One supply table at two scales. The relief crate and the courier crate are one
corporation with one warehouse; two tables would drift the first time either
was tuned and the letter would keep promising the old one.

Deliberately not incidents: the clean-up team, because a guarantee does not
admit the two questions a storyteller asks; incursion, because invariant 53
keeps exactly one door into the one named exception; and anything inside a
coordinate. All three asserted.

An assertion was wrong and the source was right, for the second time this
session. The incursion claim matched this mod's own doc comment explaining why
incursion is excluded. A proof that punishes the explanation teaches people to
delete explanations, so it now strips comments and asks about code.

A defect in the 0.11.7 ledger script was found and repaired here: its replace()
helper consumed the "Inherited pre-workflow history" heading in FINALIZED.md
instead of inserting before it. FINALIZED is append-only. No entry text was
lost; the heading is restored verbatim from HEAD~1, every insertion now
re-includes its anchor, and the diff is checked for removed lines rather than
trusted because a script printed "updated".

The heredoc \n gotcha, hit for the seventh time, with the fix already written
next to it in NOW.md.
