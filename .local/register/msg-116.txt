The second time you do a thing should be cheaper (0.11.6-dev)

Research tier 2: seven projects, one per branch, each requiring its tier 1
sibling and a completed distortion log. Tier 1 asked only for route logs
because it is about having been through once; this band is about having been
through often enough for something to have gone wrong.

Standby Discipline halves what a designated gate costs to keep while closed.
Relief Watch cuts what an unattended ramp bleeds to a quarter. Reference
Standards takes two fifths off calibration work. Known Address deepens the
familiarity discount on a coordinate the branch has dialled before.
Containment Protocol raises the aperture tier something must have before it
can follow a crew out. Forward Dispatch halves the time a shipment waits
before it leaves. Specialist Recruitment halves the hiring cooldown.

Nothing in this band supersedes anything. Every project moves a knob no other
project touches, so two cards never have to explain each other.

Three of the seven knobs the previous handoff called "already identified" were
verified hollow and replaced. stablePowerTicksRequired is 60 ticks, one
second. MaximumOpenOrders is a private sanity cap of 100 open orders.
Catalogue maxOrderQuantity is already 500 to a million per row. All three
would have PASSED proof-research-branches.py, because the capability would
have been read by real code - it would simply have moved a number no player
could observe. A live read site is not a live effect.

Two things that would have gone quiet:

- Idle draw is read in two places, what the gate reports and what it spends.
  The capability now lives in one property both call, so the readout can never
  lie about the drain.
- Forward Dispatch would have switched Relays off. The lead-time clamp kept a
  shipment from arriving before it left by reading the raw dispatch delay; it
  now measures against the effective value, or a branch holding both would
  have been pinned at the old floor.

No custom storyteller, on owner direction at the fork. A StorytellerDef is an
exclusive slot, it needs portrait art the no-new-art rule forbids, and there
is no intelligence in one to borrow. The owner's decision is both: a
guaranteed floor in our own component for the clean-up team, and an
IncidentDef surface so the player's chosen storyteller paces the lighter
world-facing events. This mod currently has zero IncidentDefs.

Register checked first: the Research and staff development family holds six
rows and none applies, because every one of them operates on
ResearchProjectDef and the vanilla research tab while these are
RimroomsProjectDef in the Operations tab.
