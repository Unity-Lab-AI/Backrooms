Zones on both sides of a gate, audited, and one defect that trapped a colonist (0.6.8-dev)

A RimWorld Zone cannot span two maps -- Zone.Map is single-valued, ZoneManager
is per-map, and the same holds for every Area. So a zone that "continues through
the gate" cannot be one object. Continuation had to mean two zones, one each
side, behaving as one: goods flow between them, work on either side attracts
somebody, and the far one's own settings are what get respected. That is the
standard every case here was held to.

The defect that made the question worth asking: a growing zone inside the
Backrooms could never be sown, and the provider held the worker there anyway.

ZoneHasWork decided sowing was wanted from three facts about the zone --
allowSow, CanAcceptSowNow, GetPlantDefToGrow -- and then treated any empty cell
as work. It never asked whether that cell could be sown. Coordinate rooms are
floored with Concrete and PavedTile; both inherit FloorBase, declare no
fertility and so carry the field default of 0, while every Core plant requires
fertilityMin of at least 0.01 and CanEverPlantAt refuses below it.

So a grower was sent, Core refused on arrival, and HasWorkHere asked the
identical question and also said yes -- the deployment was never released and
the colonist stood in the Backrooms indefinitely holding a live commitment. A
wasted crossing costs one walk. A deployment that will not release costs a
colonist.

Fixed with Core's own two gates, both of which read the cell and its own map and
take no pawn: CanEverPlantAt for terrain fertility, blockers, roof and edifices,
and PlantUtility.GrowthSeasonNow for the cell's room and temperature, which
matters independently because a coordinate has no climate control beyond
whatever the player keeps powered. Nothing is reimplemented.
zone.GetPlantDefToGrow() is used rather than WorkGiver_Grower.wantedPlantDef,
which Core writes mid-scan and a remote probe must never touch.

One hypothesis of mine was wrong and is recorded so nobody repeats it: the first
theory was that a fully roofed coordinate blocks sowing for lack of sunlight. It
does not. GrowthSeasonNow reads room and temperature, not light, and Core sows
indoors happily; plants just grow slowly without light, no differently from an
unlit greenhouse in vanilla. The real gate is terrain fertility, and building on
the light theory would have produced a check that tested the wrong thing and
still shipped the bug.

Everything else was audited rather than assumed. Stockpiles work in both
directions, with the far zone's own filter and priority deciding through
IsValidStorageFor(storeMap, thing). Fishing zones, Home areas and per-colonist
allowed areas all read the far side's own settings, the last as a recorded
observation plus the definitive check on arrival. NoRoof is deliberately emptied
inside the Backrooms by the containment rule. And zones persist across visits at
all because ShouldRemoveMapNow returns false unconditionally, so a coordinate
map is never removed -- already true by construction, now written down because
it is load-bearing and non-obvious.

Three area types have no cross-gate route and currently cannot need one:
Area_BuildRoof, Area_NoRoof, and the snow/sand and pollution clear areas. A
coordinate is already all thick rock, roof removal there is forbidden by the
world rule, and it has no outside and therefore no weather. All of them become
live the moment the far side of a gate can be an ordinary world map, so the row
is recorded as a dependency of that endpoint rather than as a free-floating gap.
