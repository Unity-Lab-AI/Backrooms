A portal is its own door cell and reserves nothing. No radius, no claimed
cells, no protected zone. The one exception is not the portal's doing at all:
linked equipment has a reach of its own, so a console or a bound battery has to
be within range. That is the equipment's constraint, and the distinction
matters - a player told "the gate needs space" builds very differently from one
told "this cabinet has to be within reach of that gate".

A REAL DEFECT, invisible from any single file. PortalEndpointRecord snapshots
the cell a traveller stands on at registration, and Availability validated that
saved cell forever after. ApproachCellFor returns the FIRST standable cardinal
neighbour, so which cell got frozen depended on what happened to be walkable
the moment the address was registered.

Build a wall on that one cell and the connection reported Obstructed for the
life of the save - with up to three other perfectly walkable cells beside the
same door, and no error, no message and no crash. A gate the design calls
permanently open that had simply stopped working because the player built a
room behind it.

Both files read correctly alone. The record's snapshotting is deliberate and
documented; the availability check honestly asks whether a cell is standable.
The bug lives only in the relationship, which is the same failure family as the
beacon that could never fire and the tier ladder that could never be climbed.

The fix is PortalEndpointRecord.TryRepairApproach(), re-deriving through
PortalAddressService.ApproachCellFor - the single derivation, whose own comment
says callers must not re-derive it.

The anchor cell is deliberately NOT refreshed. That snapshot is what stops a
moved door silently redirecting a saved route. Refreshing both would have
traded one silent failure for a worse one, and the proof asserts the asymmetry.

Not repaired while a crossing is in flight. A PortalCrossingReceipt records the
approach cells it began with and ConnectionStillMatches refuses to continue if
they changed - the guard that stops a transfer losing a pawn, invariant 55.
Moving the cell out from under a live transfer would trip that guard and abort
a legitimate crossing, so a busy edge waits, matched on the connection id
specifically. The naive fix - re-derive live everywhere - would have quietly
weakened the most safety-critical system in the mod. Enumerating every read
site before changing the value is the only reason it did not.

An eighth proof, which also enforces a forward-looking rule: no portal source
may contain ReserveCell, ClaimRadius, portalRadius, ProtectedRadius or
ReservedCells. If one ever appears, somebody has started projecting the gate
onto the map. Fault-planted four ways and correct on every one.

STILL OWED: "natruals can not be destoryed or moved". Core decides
destructibility at the DEF level - def.destroyable,
def.building.IsDeconstructible - and this mod may not change those, because it
would make every door in every colony indestructible for every player and every
other mod. Damage is solvable per-instance through the vanilla
PostPreApplyDamage comp hook. Deconstruction has no comp-level veto, and the
three honest options are all defensible, so it is queued with the question
attached rather than guessed at.
