The way out was already there (0.12.2-dev)

The guaranteed exit the direction asked for at 100%. The solo/group start now
generates two maps: an ordinary surface map holding one small concrete shell
with one door, and a REAL Backrooms coordinate beside it. The people and
everything they were carrying begin inside, and an ordinary Emergence
connection is registered at the opening rather than found by a survey draw.

A hard constraint decided the architecture, and it was found by reading
RimroomsPortalNetwork.Register rather than assumed:

    RimroomsDestinationMapParent site = secondAnchor.Map.Parent as ...;
    if (coordinate == null || coordinate.site != site || site == null || ...)
    { return PortalNetworkResult.UnknownCoordinate; }

The Backrooms side of every connection must be a real coordinate map, and the
STARTING map can never be one because Game.InitNewGame generates it for a
player Settlement and errors without one. So 0.12.0-dev's design - generating
the starting map AS the coordinate - was the thing standing in the way. The
alternative was widening the validation every existing gate depends on, which
is the riskiest change available here. Taken to the owner rather than decided
quietly; the answer was "Two maps at start, coordinate is real".

The order of the opening is its safety. Mint the coordinate, generate its map,
mark the surface door, register the connection, and move everybody inside LAST.
A failure anywhere above leaves the whole party standing safely on the surface
with no exit registered, which is recoverable. Moving them first would seal a
group inside a coordinate with no registered way out - the exact trap invariant
28 forbids. The proof asserts the ordering, and a plant that moves the party
first fails it immediately. Marking before registering for the same reason: the
network refuses an unmarked Emergence endpoint.

Why this is 100% and a survey is not: NaturalFrontierService finds a way out at
roughly one door in twelve, and only when a way home is already marked. A draw
cannot deliver a guarantee.

The surface map holds one small shell and nothing else. No power, no furniture,
no stockpile. Everything a facility needs, they build. The start def NAMES which
door is the way out, and ConfigErrors refuses a cell that is not one of its own
doors, because no door is generated there and there would be nothing to mark.

GenStep_InsideStart and the RR_InsideStart map generator are retired and
archived verbatim with the reason, per invariant 37. BuildShell stays: it is the
destination generator's own shell and the single implementation carrying
invariant 13. insideStart changed meaning and the field says so.

check-keyed-strings caught two refusal keys I invented - RR_Portal_InvalidState
and RR_Portal_LocalThresholdUnavailable. The real prefix is RR_PortalAddress_,
built by the service's own Refuse helper. A refusal key that does not exist
shows the player the raw key.

Fault-planted three ways: the party moved before registration, the door marked
after registration, and the emergence cell moved off the start's own door.
Correct on all three. The starts proof now holds over three starts, three
layouts, the opening sequence and the natural chain.
