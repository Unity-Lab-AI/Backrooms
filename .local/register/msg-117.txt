The corporation does not write off a branch (0.11.7-dev)

The clean-up team, and the guaranteed-floor half of the owner's decision at the
storyteller fork: "Both - guaranteed floor, storyteller flavour".

A branch in corporation contact that loses every living staff member gets a
clean-up team on all-access passes. Hostiles are removed from the site, five
replacement staff are requisitioned one per company role, and a basic supply
drop lands with them. No cap and no escalation: the corporation "will put up
with anything", and a rescue that gets stingier is a deadline wearing a
different hat.

Deliberately not an IncidentDef. A storyteller decides whether and when to
fire one, and "facilities never die" admits neither question. The storyteller
half is a separate surface and is next.

Five PawnKindDefs were already written and read by nothing. The whole RR_*Staff
set - operations, research, engineering, security, medical logistics - authored
with skill ranges matching the five company roles exactly, loaded and validated
every run, mentioned by no source file. The queue called them "unbuilt". They
were built and orphaned, which is more dangerous, because an orphaned def looks
finished from every angle except the one nobody checks. Third instance of the
same shape this session.

Three decisions inside it:

- It fires on "no employed staff alive ANYWHERE", never "alive here". A crew
  standing in a Backrooms coordinate is alive and the facility is not dead, so
  a player who takes everybody through a gate never comes home to a relief team
  and a duplicate payroll. Downed is also not dead, which closes the obvious
  exploit by definition rather than by a counter-measure.
- Hostiles are removed, not killed. A clean-up team that left forty corpses on
  the floor of a facility with nobody in it to haul them has not cleaned
  anything up, and the rot would land on the replacement crew.
- reliefCount is a record and lastReliefTick is a timestamp. The proof asserts
  nothing compares either against a limit.

Core facts read from the decompiled assembly rather than remembered:
GameEnder.gameEnding is a public field, so no Harmony is needed; Core clears it
itself whenever a map holds a free colonist; the game-over countdown is 400
ticks and the relief is checked every 60.

Not routed through the hiring pipeline, whose job is matching onboarding
receipts against a saved quote. There is no charge here - the corporation pays
for its own rescue - and using that path would have meant inventing a receipt.

Register checked first across world threat and event pacing, faction standing,
scenario setup and recovery systems. Row 199, Safely Hidden Away, changes how
the game reasons about colonists who are not present, which is exactly the
state this must not mistake for a dead facility; that rule is asserted rather
than commented.

A fifth proof, fault-planted four ways and correct on every one.
