Somebody finally runs the bill on the far side (0.6.5-dev)

Ingredients have crossed a gate to a bill since 0.5.7-dev and nobody was ever
sent to work the bench. A coordinate holding a stove, a live bill and delivered
ingredients produced nothing unless a colonist happened to be standing there.
The supply half was settled; this half had never been decided.

The Core fact the carry family pinned as an obstacle turns out to enable this
one. ClosestUnfinishedThingForBill validates Creator == pawn and
Bill_ProductionWithUft binds BoundUft to a BoundWorker, so a half-made thing
belongs to exactly one colonist -- which is why the carry family must never
touch one, and why a deployed worker running Core's own WorkGiver_DoBill on the
bill's own map is correct by construction rather than as a workaround.

Built as five families, not one, because Core draws that line itself:
StartOrResumeBillJob compares bill.recipe.requiredGiverWorkType against
def.workType, and a bench belongs to a work type only through
WorkGiverDef.fixedBillGiverDefs. A single provider would have declared one work
type and pulled a cook across a gate for smithing. Cooking, Crafting, Smithing,
Tailoring and Art each get their own provider, priority pair and work-settings
row. Twenty-seven families now, nineteen of them deployments.

The bench set is unioned from the loaded giver defs rather than named, so a mod
adding a bench to an existing work type is covered with no code here and no
mention of it. Match by capability, one level deeper than usual.

Rules split by what each actually reads, verified in source. PawnAllowedToStartAnew
is safe to ask remotely -- it reads the bill's pawn restriction, its slaves-only
and mechs-only flags and the pawn's skill against allowedSkillRange, and none of
it touches the pawn's map -- so a bill restricted to one named colonist or to a
skill band never drags the wrong worker through a gate. nextTickToSearchForIngredients
is read and never written. TryFindBestBillIngredients, every reservation, the
interaction cell and the whole unfinished-thing resolution stay at arrival.

One test is deliberately approximate and says so: every ingredient must be
present within the bill's radius, which is necessary and not sufficient. It
exists because a coordinate with a bench, a live bill and no materials would
otherwise look like work forever -- Core's own throttle only moves when a pawn
tries and fails, and on a map nobody stands on nobody ever tries.

Also fixes a defect shipped five checkpoints ago. ConnectedBillAdapter has said
since 0.5.7-dev that autonomous and mech bills are out of scope, while filtering
with `if (!(bill is Bill_Production))`. But Bill_Autonomous derives from
Bill_Production and Bill_Mech from Bill_Autonomous, so both passed straight
through the test written to exclude them, and the carry family has been
supplying mech gestator bills without review. Both families now share one type
test in ConnectedBillScan, which excludes Bill_Autonomous explicitly. Found by
reading the hierarchy instead of trusting the names.
