The escalation ladder, paced against colony wealth (0.8.0-dev)

The ladder had been specified since 2026-09-28 with one question deliberately
left open: what does pressure scale against? The owner has answered it -- colony
wealth -- and that is the right answer for a RimWorld mod, because wealth is the
input the game's own storyteller uses. A coordinate now paces against the same
curve as everything else the player is facing rather than running a private
difficulty track alongside it.

Wealth raises the ceiling and never the floor. The history terms -- how many
times a coordinate has been visited and how long the branch's people have worked
inside it -- are saved against the coordinate. The wealth term only ever clamps
them. So a colony that gets rich makes deep spaces able to become dangerous, but
can never retroactively make a space the player already knows more dangerous than
its own recorded history earned.

Wealth is read from player home maps only. A Backrooms map full of generated
furniture is not something the player earned, and counting it would make a space
escalate simply because it was well stocked.

The three failure modes named in the original direction are avoided as properties
of the function rather than as discipline expected of its callers. BandFor reads
nothing but fixed and saved terms: it does not read the clock, does not roll, and
does not ask how many gates are open. A revisit therefore resumes rather than
rerolling up to punish it or down to make it safe, and several open gates never
sum, because each coordinate carries its own history and is evaluated alone.

The owner's condition that a solo group must be able to build, supply and find a
way out is treated as an acceptance condition on the arithmetic rather than a
hope, so three guarantees live in the file instead of in tuning. Three
simultaneous encounters is an absolute cap at any depth and any wealth, which is
a constant rather than a curve precisely because it is the number keeping the
condition true. Half of every coordinate's rooms present nothing, chosen by a
count from the coordinate's seed rather than by chance, so an unlucky run of
rolls can never produce a space with something in every room. And a first visit
is always quiet: whatever the colony is worth and however deep the space, walking
in is never the dangerous part. Shallow coordinates are additionally capped below
the top band regardless of wealth, because the shallow Backrooms is where a solo
start has to be able to operate.

This repo has a documented failure mode -- public APIs with no callers, built and
compiling and reachable by nothing -- so the quiet-room guarantee was wired
straight into generation in the same checkpoint rather than left for later. Room
dressing now asks the ladder before dressing, and half of every coordinate's
rooms are genuinely bare.

A visit is recorded on the empty-to-occupied transition on a Backrooms map rather
than on a gate opening. The rule is that pressure rises from operating history at
that coordinate, and a gate opened onto a space nobody walks into is not
operating history. It also means the count cannot be inflated by cycling a gate
from the safe side.
