# Walkthrough distillation: defence and combat (RimWorld 1.5/1.6/1.7)
Format: one rule per line. [family] rule -- source. W+Page = https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Page (distilled from guide knowledge; numbers not live-verified this pass)

## Raid recognition
1. [war] Read the raid letter: "raid" with no arrival note = walk-in edge raid; "drop pods" = pods may land anywhere incl. inside base; "siege"/"camp" = enemies dig in at the map edge -- W+Raid
2. [war] Raid points scale with colony wealth (items+buildings) and colonist/animal combat power; keep wealth low early and grow defence before wealth -- W+Raid_points
3. [war] First raid normally arrives around day 5-10 under the default storyteller; have a defended position by day 5 -- W+Storytellers
4. [war] Threats are measured as alert_* in sensors; treat any hostile pawn on map (mechs, insects, manhunters included) as threat=true -- W+Raid
5. [war] Raid strategies: Immediate attack, Stage then attack (waits ~hours at edge), Siege, Sappers (dig through walls), Breachers (blast through walls), Drop pods edge, Drop pods center -- W+Raid
6. [war] Sapper/breacher raids ignore killbox entrances; keep a fallback interior ring and draft to meet them at the breach -- W+Raid

## Tribal raids
7. [war] Tribals come in large numbers with melee and bows; fight from cover at range and let them close across open ground -- W+Tribal
8. [war] Use melee blockers in 1-tile doorways to choke tribal melee swarms -- W+Combat
9. [war] Tribals flee after ~50% losses; stop pursuing past the base perimeter -- W+Raid

## Pirate / outlander raids
10. [war] Pirates carry guns and grenades; never stand clustered within 3 tiles of each other versus grenades -- W+Pirate
11. [war] Fight pirates from full cover (walls/embrasures 75%, sandbags 55%, rocks/chunks 50%, trees 25%) -- W+Cover
12. [war] Prioritise killing grenadiers, snipers and rocket launcher carriers first -- W+Defense_tactics

## Mechanoid raids
13. [war] Mechanoids do not flee, are immune to fire/temperature and toxic; plan to kill all -- W+Mechanoids
14. [war] Use EMP grenades/EMP mortar shells on mechanoids: EMP stuns and drains shields -- W+EMP_grenades
15. [war] Never melee a Scyther/Lancer team with untrained pawns; Centipedes are slow -- kite and focus them with high-armour-penetration weapons -- W+Centipede
16. [war] Lure mechanoids into the killbox; they hit hard on walls -- use stone (granite best HP) walls, not wood -- W+Wall
17. [war] Mech cluster (Royalty) = stationary; do not wake it until ready; destroy the mech assembler/turrets with mortars or a full-force assault -- W+Mech_cluster

## Siege raids
18. [war] Siege = enemies build sandbags and mortars at the edge; do not wait inside -- attack once they've spread out, or counter-mortar -- W+Raid#Siege
19. [war] Siege camps leave steel and mortar shells; loot them after the fight -- W+Raid#Siege
20. [war] Roofing (thick mountain roof) blocks mortar shells; mountain base is siege-proof -- W+Roof

## Drop pod raids
21. [war] Drop pods (center) land inside walls; keep weapons on every colonist and draft immediately on letter -- W+Raid
22. [war] Drop pods cannot land under overhead mountain (thick roof); this is the main reason to build the main base into a mountain -- W+Roof

## Infestations
23. [war] Infestations spawn only under overhead mountain/rock roof in areas at temperature above -17C, preferring dark, unused spaces -- W+Infestation
24. [war] Keep unused mountain rooms lit and cooled; a freezer at -18C or below blocks infestation spawns there -- W+Infestation
25. [war] Kill infestations with fire (molotovs/incendiary) or lock the hive room and let it starve; hives keep spawning insects, destroy hives first -- W+Hive
26. [war] Insect jelly drops from hives; keep it (it never spoils) -- W+Insect_jelly

## Manhunters
27. [war] Manhunter packs wander and attack anyone outside; on letter set all colonists to a safe zone (Home area / allowed area indoors) and close doors -- W+Manhunter
28. [war] Manhunter animals cannot open doors; wait them out, they recover after ~1-2 days or shoot them from behind walls/through doors -- W+Manhunter
29. [war] After killing manhunters, butcher the corpses fast (meat before rot, ~1-2 days unrefrigerated) -- W+Manhunter

## Killbox
30. [base-and-fortress] Build the killbox as a single long tunnel/funnel the enemy must walk through; all other perimeter fully walled -- W+Killbox
31. [base-and-fortress] Pathing rule: raiders prefer an open path; if all routes are walled they dig/break walls -- always leave exactly one open route -- W+Killbox
32. [base-and-fortress] Killbox length 10-20 tiles of open ground under fire; shooters behind embrasures/sandbags at the end -- W+Killbox
33. [base-and-fortress] Build a zig-zag or maze in the entrance to lengthen time-under-fire -- W+Killbox
34. [base-and-fortress] Place traps (spike traps, IEDs) in the funnel; colonists know trap locations, raiders do not -- W+Trap
35. [base-and-fortress] Use doors in the killbox so friendly traffic passes; set door "hold open" off -- W+Door
36. [base-and-fortress] Keep the killbox floor clear of chunks/trees (they give enemy cover); remove cover in the funnel -- W+Cover

## Embrasures / sandbags / barricades
37. [base-and-fortress] Sandbags: 55% cover, built from textile (cloth/leather); line every shooter position with them -- W+Sandbags
38. [base-and-fortress] Barricades: stone/wood, 55% cover, buildable from any material -- W+Barricade
39. [base-and-fortress] Embrasures (1.3+): 75% cover, walls with gaps shooters fire through; enemies cannot path through them -- W+Embrasure
40. [base-and-fortress] Stand colonists directly behind a sandbag/embrasure (adjacent tile) to get cover; cover only counts against shots from the far side -- W+Cover
41. [base-and-fortress] Early (day 1-5) without steel: wood walls + wood barricades, replace with stone ASAP (wood burns) -- W+Wall

## Turrets
42. [base-and-fortress] Mini-turret: needs Gun turrets research, steel + components, 80W power, needs rearming periodically -- W+Mini-turret
43. [base-and-fortress] Turrets explode on destruction (~3 tile radius); never place adjacent to each other or colonists -- W+Mini-turret
44. [base-and-fortress] Place turrets behind sandbags/walls, out of the first sight line, to back up colonists not replace them -- W+Turret
45. [base-and-fortress] Turrets draw first fire; use them as distraction in front of colonist line -- W+Turret
46. [base-and-fortress] Mortars need Mortar research + shells; useful vs sieges and mech clusters, not vs fast raids -- W+Mortar

## Draft tactics
47. [war] Draft all combat-capable colonists on raid letter; keep doctors and non-violent pawns indoors -- W+Draft
48. [war] Shoot at max range of the weapon; let enemies walk into accuracy falloff -- W+Ranged_combat
49. [war] Focus fire one target at a time; downed > dead for prisoners -- W+Combat
50. [war] Melee blocker: a high-armour melee pawn stands in a 1-wide door; shooters fire past from diagonal -- W+Combat
51. [war] Never let colonists fight in the open without cover; never chase fleeing raiders far -- W+Defense_tactics
52. [war] Use the Home area/zones: set allowed area to "indoors" during raids for non-combatants -- W+Allowed_area
53. [war] Undraft after "raid defeated" and immediately rescue downed colonists (bleeding: ~1 day to death; tend wounds first) -- W+Bleeding

## Kiting
54. [war] Kite melee enemies with faster pawns: shoot, step back out of reach, shoot again -- W+Kiting
55. [war] Kite slow mechs (Centipede move speed low) with rifles; never kite Scythers (fast) -- W+Centipede
56. [war] Kite manhunter animals only with long range weapons from behind a door -- W+Manhunter

## Fallback
57. [war] Fall back to an inner defensible room if outer line breaks; keep a second ring with doors -- W+Defense_tactics
58. [war] If raid points are overwhelming (enemy count > 3x colonists early), fall back indoors and let turrets/traps work -- W+Defense_tactics
59. [war] Keep a medicine stock (>= 10 medicine) and a hospital with beds near the killbox -- W+Medicine
60. [war] Fire: if enemies use incendiaries, keep firefoam poppers; put out fires inside base first -- W+Fire

## Prisoners
61. [war] Build a prison room (min 1 bed per prisoner, walled, door, roofed) before the first fight -- W+Prisoner
62. [war] Down enemies (do not execute); capture with colonist "Capture" order; tend prisoners' wounds -- W+Prisoner
63. [war] Set prisoner interaction "Reduce resistance" then "Recruit"; use warden with high social -- W+Prisoner
64. [war] Prisoner room temp 16-26C, minimal comfort; feed them -- W+Prisoner
65. [war] Release prisoners to improve faction relations (+~15 goodwill) if not recruiting -- W+Prisoner
66. [war] Strip weapons/armour of downed enemies and store in a stockpile; sell or reuse -- W+Combat

## PLC thresholds (decision points)
67. [meta] threat=true -> draft all fighters within 1 tick; non-fighters to safe area -- W+Draft
68. [meta] day >= 3 -> stone walls around the camp; day >= 5 -> killbox entrance and sandbags in place -- W+Killbox
69. [meta] research Gun turrets done + steel >= 140 + components >= 3 -> build 2 mini-turrets -- W+Mini-turret
70. [meta] infestation risk: unused mountain room temp > -17C -> light it or turn it into freezer at -18C -- W+Infestation
71. [meta] downed colonist + raid over -> rescue + tend within 1 day; medicine < 10 -> raise priority on herbal medicine/medicine production -- W+Medicine
72. [meta] siege detected -> attack before mortar placement or counter-mortar -- W+Raid
73. [meta] manhunter alert -> restrict allowed area to indoors until alert clears (~1-2 days) -- W+Manhunter
