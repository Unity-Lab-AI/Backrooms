# Walkthrough distillation -- Crashlanded opening and first camp (days 0-5)
Sources: B = https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Basics ; Q = https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Quickstart_Guides
One rule per line: imperative, [family], source. Thresholds a PLC can read are marked THRESH.

## Landing (hour 0)
- Pause on landing, before any order. [setup] Q
- Unforbid every pod item and drop with Allow (F) at once; THRESH: forbidden starting items > 0 -> run unforbid. [start] Q
- Give the bolt-action rifle to the best shooter, the revolver to the next, and the knife to the best melee pawn. [start] B,Q
- Wear the helmet; skip the flak vest and flak pants early because they slow movement. [start] Q
- Turn on manual work priorities first (they are off by default). [configuration] B,Q
- Set Firefight, Patient, Bed rest and Basic to 1 for every pawn. [configuration] B,Q
- Set Hauling to 1 for everyone in the opening, then lower it once the pod drop is stored. [configuration] B,Q
- Set Doctor, Cook, Construct and Grow to 1 for the best-skilled pawn in each, and disable them for the others. [configuration] Q
- Set work the pawn is passionate about to 2 and all other work to 4. [configuration] Q
- Set Mine to 2 and Research to 4. [configuration] B
- Give Crafting/stonecutting to whichever pawn is otherwise idle. [configuration] B
- Scout the map for chokepoints, ruins and existing walls before you choose the site. [start] Q
- Settle near the landing spot; do not haul everything across the map. [start] Q
- Start with 450 steel; do not spend steel on walls -- build in wood. [economy] Q

## Day 1 before nightfall
- Chop trees on the site first, then chop more for wood stock. [start] B,Q
- Lay one stockpile zone over the whole camp floor and haul everything into it. [start] B,Q
- Store everything under a roof except steel and stone blocks; everything else deteriorates outside over time. [start] B,Q
- Build one wooden room: 7x7 minimum (about 50 interior tiles, 7x7 or 6x8), 9x9 is better, with 1-3 wooden doors. [start] B,Q
- Enclose the room fully; the roof is added for free once walls close it. [start] B
- THRESH: a room only holds temperature when it is enclosed and more than 75% of its interior is roofed. [survival] B
- Build in this order: walls and door, then beds, then a 1x2 table and a stool. [start] B
- Put 3 wooden beds in (one per pawn) before the first night. [start] Q
- THRESH: a pawn sleeping without a bed takes -4 mood (slept on the ground); beds needed = colonist count. [survival] B
- Put a campfire in the middle of the room for heat and light (outside if the weather is very hot). [survival] Q
- Use a torch lamp (10 wood, burns 10 days) for light before power. [start] B
- Have every pawn under a roof and in a bed by night 1. [survival] Q
- Plant 30-40 crop tiles on day 1. [growth] Q
- Forbid low-priority blueprints and unforbid them when materials are ready. [start] B

## Sleeping
- Do not give orders to sleeping pawns; it causes disturbed sleep. [survival] Q
- Queue next-morning jobs with shift-click instead of waking pawns. [meta] Q
- Check moods in the Needs tab on night 1. [survival] Q
- Add end tables and dressers by beds later for comfort; a normal bed saves about 1 hour of sleep a day. [base-and-fortress] B
- THRESH: keep the bedroom near 21 C; without heat expect -4 (slept in the cold). [survival] Q

## Food
- Count on the starting packaged survival meals as the first-week buffer (about 50 meals, about 8 days for 3). [survival] B
- THRESH: a pawn eats 2 simple meals a day; plan 6 meals/day for 3 pawns. [survival] B
- THRESH: uncooked a pawn needs 32 raw food a day; cooked, 20 raw food a day (10 raw -> 1 simple meal). [survival] B,Q
- THRESH: pawns starve after just under 3 days with no food. [survival] B
- Size crops at 25 tiles (5x5) per pawn on normal soil, 20 per pawn on rich soil (75 / 60 tiles for 3). [growth] B
- Make four 5x5 grow zones: 2 rice, 1 strawberry, 1 potato to start. [growth] Q
- Prefer rich soil (+40% growth); check the fertility overlay. [growth] B,Q
- Plant rice first (fastest); switch to potatoes after the first rice/strawberry harvest, later 2 potato + 2 corn. [growth] B,Q
- Add cotton, corn and healroot zones in the next few days. [growth] Q
- Harvest wild berries and healroot when ripe. [growth] Q
- Hunt and gather to bridge the gap until the first harvest. [animals] Q
- Never hunt boomrats, boomalopes, pack animals or predators. [animals] Q
- Butcher fresh corpses only; rotten corpses cannot be butchered. [animals] Q
- THRESH: raw food (except berries) gives -7 mood -- cook it. [survival] Q
- Build a wooden butcher table and a fueled stove near the freezer. [base-and-fortress] Q
- Set the stove bill to simple meals, do until you have about 6 (3 pawns) or 10-20, with pause until satisfied. [survival] B,Q
- THRESH: meals rot in about 4 days; keep only 1-2 days of meals cooked. [survival] B
- THRESH: a cook under skill 3 or a dirty kitchen raises food poisoning; floor the kitchen. [survival] Q
- Skip the nutrient paste dispenser early. [survival] Q
- THRESH: freezer must be below 0 C; set the cooler to -5 C (default target is 21 C). [base-and-fortress] B,Q
- Make the freezer about 7x7 with one door, cooler blue side in, red side out. [base-and-fortress] Q
- Freezer stockpile: fresh food, corpses, potatoes, herbal medicine; forbid rotten; priority important. [base-and-fortress] Q
- THRESH: indoor unfrozen shelf life -- rice 40 days, corn 60, potatoes 30. [survival] B
- THRESH: store at least 1000 food before winter. [survival] Q
- Use shelves for 3 stacks a tile; stone chunks cannot go on shelves. [base-and-fortress] B,Q

## Research
- Build a simple research bench in the first few days (about day 3, once the room and beds are done). [research] B,Q
- Light the bench with a standing lamp (darkness gives a mood penalty). [research] Q
- Research first: Battery (400) then Solar panel (600). [research] B,Q
- Research Watermill generator (700) first instead when the map has a river. [research] B,Q
- Then Smithing (700) -> Machining (1000); Gunsmithing (500) -> Blowback operation (500) -> Gas operation (1000). [research] B
- Leave Microelectronics (3000) and Geothermal (3200) for later. [research] B

## Power (after day 3)
- Use a wood-fired generator for a steady 1000 W (needs wood refuelling); wind turbines fluctuate. [base-and-fortress] Q
- THRESH: conduit link range is 6 tiles. [base-and-fortress] Q
- Vent generator heat with a remove-roof area. [base-and-fortress] Q

## Safety, fire, first fight
- Cut grass around buildings to stop fire spread (dry thunderstorms start fires). [survival] B
- Clear the home area so pawns stay inside the walls. [base-and-fortress] B
- Expect the first threat to be one animal or one raider with a basic weapon. [war] Q
- Draft shooters behind cover; the melee pawn intercepts anything that reaches them. [war] Q
- THRESH: cover -- walls up to 75%, sandbags/barricades 55%, stone chunks 50%, trees 25%; melee ignores cover. [war] Q
- Leave a wall gap with traps as a funnel; raiders do not break doors, they path round or hit random walls. [war] B
- Treat minor wounds with no medicine; save medicine for disease and moderate/extreme blood loss. [survival] B,Q
- Keep the doctor out of the front line; injured pawns rest in bed (Bed rest priority). [survival] Q
- Bury human corpses in a grave (seen corpses cost mood); stripped clothing is tainted. [survival] Q
- Give a new colonist work priorities and a bed the same day. [start] Q
- Recruit prisoners only if useful (no addiction, no chronic illness, has skills). [growth] Q

## After the first days
- Build a stonecutter's table and replace wooden walls with stone. [base-and-fortress] B,Q
- THRESH: a roof needs a wall/door/support within 6 tiles or it collapses. [base-and-fortress] Q
- Cook fine meals (+5 mood) from meat plus vegetables. [survival] Q
