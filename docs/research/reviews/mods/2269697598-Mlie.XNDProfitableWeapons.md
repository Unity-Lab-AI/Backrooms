## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes configurable weapon sale prices, raising the normal multiplier from 20% to a default 100%; looted weapons receive a `(U)` tag and a separate reduced multiplier, with stockpile filters. It claims compatibility with Combat Extended, MendAndRecycle, and Nano Repair Tech. The page describes how origin/use affects the tag and notes existing saves receive flags only for newly generated weapons; removal may flicker. These remain publisher claims, not reproduced behavior.

**Provisional disposition:** optional economy configuration/no touch. Rimrooms' company ledger must not double-credit ordinary weapon sales. Related features: `RR-ECO`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-COMPAT`.
