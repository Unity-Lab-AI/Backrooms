# RimWorld mod review: Social Experience from Trade

- Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400608063
- Linked source: https://github.com/Krelinos/Social-EXP-From-Trade
- Workshop ID: `3400608063`; local package ID: `Krelinos.SocialEXPFromTrade`
- Review date: 2026-09-27; local version not declared; local support declaration 1.3–1.6
- Local and publisher metadata require Harmony and load after Harmony. No other DLC, ordering, or conflict requirement is declared. Upstream declares MIT.

## Verified source facts

The publisher says buying experience is based by default on market value and selling experience on negotiated value; settings control amounts. Royalty tribute transactions do not award experience. Experience is granted at trade confirmation and adds no items. Workshop comments report large level jumps and a possible high-Social pricing oddity; these are user reports, not reproduced results. A publisher statement about a 300-mod playthrough is not evidence for this exact profile.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional staff-skill progression; no direct Rimrooms ledger bridge.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-ECO; RR-COMPAT; RR-MP`.
- Keep experience separate from company money, research ownership, and RWT branch progression. A normal transaction must never pay twice because this mod grants pawn experience.
- Future check: buy/sell negotiator, skills/passions/settings, large trades and high Social, Royalty tribute, cart-altering mods, then RWT inter-branch trade for branch-local skills and duplicate-safe accounting.
