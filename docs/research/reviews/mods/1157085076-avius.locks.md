## Verified source facts

The publisher describes flick-to-lock/unlock controls, passage rules for humans/enemies/prisoners, visitor exclusion, pet doors for small animals, and per-colonist door restrictions. Disabling on an existing save requires clearing lock/unlock jobs. The page warns that custom door security may conflict and describes a restriction when using Lockable Doors. Steam comments mention 1.6 pathfinding/prison-passage concerns and ask about RWT; these are unverified user reports and not evidence of a known failure or RWT support.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional access-control complement; no Rimrooms security guarantee should depend on it.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-THREAT; RR-STA; RR-COMPAT; RR-MP`.
- Ensure ordinary facility access and containment remain understandable and functional without this optional door mod. Do not use it as the sole proof that a Backrooms gate is secure.
- Future check: vanilla and mod doors with colonists, visitors, prisoners, animals, and mechs; jobs/flicking/pathing/escape; Doors Expanded, Prison Labor, Imprisonment On The Go, custom gates/airlocks, save removal, map transitions, and RWT.

No door, custody, save-removal, full-profile, or RWT test was run. Workshop comments and release-version differences remain unresolved leads.
