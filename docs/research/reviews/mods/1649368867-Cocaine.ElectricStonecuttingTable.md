## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a powered stonecutting table working at 175% of normal speed; when power is short it becomes slower than an unpowered bench. It requires Stonecutting and Electricity plus a separate configurable research (default 500, may be disabled). The page says some third-party recipes may need a manual patch. Stonecutting Extended (row 219) is a selected-profile interaction candidate.
