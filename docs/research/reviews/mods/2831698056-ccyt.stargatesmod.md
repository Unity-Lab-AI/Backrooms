## Verified source facts

- The publisher describes three gate variants, glyph/site discovery, gate-to-gate planetary travel, raids through gates, iris defense, cargo/pawn transfer, and automatic closure after roughly one in-game hour without traffic. Caravan-created named gate sites are also described.
- The checked upstream license is GPL-3.0. Do not copy its code, definitions, graphics, sounds, or exact content into Rimrooms' planned MIT package.
- The source surface covers world sites, pawns, cargo, quests, and arrival behavior. No runtime tests were run.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional:** exclude as a gameplay dependency or direct integration target; retain only as a high-level mechanics reference.
- Related systems: `RR-GATE`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-MSN`, `RR-COMPAT`; see the [feature map](../../../FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md).
- Its freely available planetary gate travel and short idle timeout do not match Rimrooms' powered, limited-duration machine gate and persistent Backrooms coordinates. Build Rimrooms' gate and generated spaces independently; keep the Core campaign playable without it.
