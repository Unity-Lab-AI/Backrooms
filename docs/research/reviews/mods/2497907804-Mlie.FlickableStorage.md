## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes four storage states: on, off, accept-only, and retrieve-only. Settings propagate to linked storage. Retrieval blocking relies on forbidden checks that mental-break pawns ignore; chunks cannot be forbidden. The publisher says it should support storage using `IHaulDestination` and reports tests with LWM's original Deep Storage, which does not certify this profile's ASF ports. Its multiplayer credit refers to a different multiplayer mod, not specifically RWT.

**Provisional disposition:** optional storage configuration; do not use for security, custody, or ownership enforcement. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-COMPAT`.
