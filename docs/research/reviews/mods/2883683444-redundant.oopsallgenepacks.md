## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes larger gene archives/registers and gene processors that raise complexity limits at added material, power, and space cost. Optional direct trader access can expose stored genes; the page mentions compatibility with Biotech Cloning.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional biology-lab/storage reference; no Rimrooms dependency.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-EVD; RR-ECO; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- The selected Orbit Trade mod (row 154) says it removes gene-bank postfixes and invalidates this mod's `visibleToTraders` setting; this is publisher-documented, not runtime-confirmed. Test capacities, processors, power, trader toggle/stock, orbital trade, save/load, and custom gene transfer. Do not assume RWT handles custom gene contents.

Keep Biotech optional and do not treat stored genes as Rimrooms evidence without a separately scoped bridge.
