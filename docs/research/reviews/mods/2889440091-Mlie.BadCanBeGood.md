## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says it rebalances vanilla traits, pairing some drawbacks with benefits and changing pawn difficulty/market value. Its examples include work-speed effects for Ugly and medical/movement effects for Wimp.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional pawn-balance change; hiring, staff value, and work roles remain independent.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** trait effects and market value in isolation; then full profile with trait generation, recruitment, and economy. If pawn visits/exchanges are supported, confirm that hiring and value calculations stay local to each branch.

No combined-profile or RWT behavior was tested.
