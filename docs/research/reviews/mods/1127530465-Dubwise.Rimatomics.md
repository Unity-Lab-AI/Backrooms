## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use its native features as optional player-facing power/security content; do not require Rimatomics to build or run the machine gate, and do not patch its research system before the interaction checks.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-GATE`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep Rimrooms power draw, gate state, progression, and security readable when Rimatomics is absent. Preserve the current load ordering with `Do Your F****** Research` ahead of its research benches.
