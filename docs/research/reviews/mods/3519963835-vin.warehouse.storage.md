A workshop discussion reports boxes moving or damaging nearby structures when gravships launch/rotate, with a comment saying the game fix did not cover rotation. These are reports, not reproduced results. This mod meets ASF (10), LWM Adaptive Deep Storage (122), and VGE gravship content (247/281).

**Provisional disposition:** optional installed storage/no touch. Keep it out of any required Rimrooms path until assets and interactions are understood. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
