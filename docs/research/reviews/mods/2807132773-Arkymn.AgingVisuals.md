# RimWorld mod review: Graying Hair

- Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2807132773
- Row 102; package `Arkymn.AgingVisuals`; local version blank; support 1.3–1.6; Harmony required. Local order includes Core and the five DLCs plus VPE; VPE is not selected. No local conflicts.
- No source/license verified. Reviewed 2026-09-27; no runtime test.

## Source facts and Rimrooms use

Publisher says hair begins graying at age 40 and turns white by 60 by default, with configurable thresholds. It updates on birthdays, does not reveal hidden Anomaly pawns, and has optional Chronophagy, VPE and Biosculpter interactions. Reverse graying needs Biotech; the publisher says base graying does not. Grayless-hair genes prevent it; agelessness alone does not. Humanoid species use life expectancy for the calculation.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional staff appearance flavor; no adapter.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STYLE; RR-STA; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** thresholds/birthdays, genes, agelessness, dyed/gradient hair, optional Biotech reversal; ensure Core-only works with this absent.

User reports and the publisher's birthday explanation were not reproduced.
