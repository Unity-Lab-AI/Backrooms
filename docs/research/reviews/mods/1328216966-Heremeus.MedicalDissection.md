## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a Dissection research project/table, Medicine skill training, adjustable XP/organ-loss chances, and single-use corpses that may lose organs/body parts. It also describes a Harvest Organs Post Mortem patch that allows autopsy-table dissection and changes the prerequisite. Most importantly, the Workshop page's **Multiplayer support** section explicitly states that the mod does not support multiplayer and will likely cause desyncs. This is a publisher warning, not a desync reproduced in this review; the local manifest's empty incompatibility field does not negate it. No DLC hard requirement was found.

**Provisional disposition:** optional content. The owner selected this mod for the RWT candidate test despite the publisher warning; this does not certify it as compatible or include it in the required co-op stack. Do not make dissection a core evidence, research, or corpse-processing route. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-MP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
