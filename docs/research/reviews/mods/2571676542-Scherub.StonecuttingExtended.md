## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes bulk stonecutting, configurable work amounts and skill XP, and selecting Artistic, Construction or Crafting skill. Bulk recipes can appear on benches defined as `TableStonecutter`; the page names support for selected third-party stone definitions. Removing the mod can lose saved bulk bills and produce a one-time error. Row 88 Electric Stonecutting Table is a selected interaction candidate, not a known conflict.

**Provisional disposition:** optional material-processing expansion; keep vanilla stonecutting available. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-COMPAT`.
