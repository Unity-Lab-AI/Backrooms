## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says it patches overhead mountain into removable thin rock roof. It cannot be rebuilt as the same mountain roof; the resulting roof permits mortar/drop-pod impacts and suppresses the patched mountain-infestation effect. The page recommends loading the patch last and says it changes definitions at startup.

**Provisional disposition:** optional profile utility only; do not bundle or depend on it. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-SPACE`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`, `RR-DLC`. Do not assume a mountain roof preserves normal protection after removal.
