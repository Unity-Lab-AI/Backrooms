## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes building mass from material mass and configurable construction efficiency. MinifyEverything or similar is recommended, not a hard dependency. The repository claims DLC support but says it tested only Royalty and Ideology.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional logistics/balance companion; no gate or expedition dependency.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-EXP; RR-OUT; RR-SPACEFLIGHT; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** mass calculations/settings, building minify/reinstall; selected building transport, cargo, gravships, and RWT item exchange. Do not generalize repository compatibility claims to this profile.

No source files or assets are included in Rimrooms.
