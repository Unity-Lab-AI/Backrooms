## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a wall-mounted monitor with the efficiency of a regular vitals monitor and a mounting-component cost. It names patches for VFE Medical Module and Altered Carbon 2, neither selected. In a page comment the author says a stacking issue like regular monitors may occur and they know no way to prevent it; this is a publisher comment, not a reproduced result.

**Provisional disposition:** optional compact medical furniture; keep treatment usable without it. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-COMPAT`.
