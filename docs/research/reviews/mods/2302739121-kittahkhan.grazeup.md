## Verified source facts

- The mod changes animal grazing so consumed plants can regrow, and optionally lets animals graze plants covered by snow. Plant nutrition and lifespan settings are configurable.
- The Workshop page distinguishes grazing from harvesting; it does not change crop harvest rules.
- It affects animal feeding and plant growth, not Rimrooms' company records. No code or assets are copied.
