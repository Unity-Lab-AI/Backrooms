## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says caravan trade purchases and guest gifts are placed into reachable storage where possible; items are dropped if no reachable space exists or the caravan is leaving. It names Common Sense's option to put trade items into a pawn's inventory as its only known conflict. Common Sense is in the selected profile (row 66).

**Provisional disposition:** optional delivery convenience/no touch. Ground-drop fallback must remain understandable and paid goods must not be duplicated or lost. Related features: `RR-ECO`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
