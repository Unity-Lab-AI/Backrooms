## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says it suppresses the Medical Emergency alert for extreme blood loss only when a prisoner's interaction is Hemogen Farm or Bloodfeed; other life-threatening conditions still alert. The author documents an edge case when arresting a pawn already at critical blood loss and notes a possible small alert-check cost with many prisoners. It was updated for 1.6; a Steam removal banner gives no reason.

**Provisional disposition:** optional alert-only patch, never a treatment or containment system. Biotech is required for this optional mod. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
