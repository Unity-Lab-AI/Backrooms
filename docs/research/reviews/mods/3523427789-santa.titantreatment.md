## Verified source facts

The publisher describes a Judge Dredd-inspired surgery unlocked after Bionics and costing 20 Plasteel plus 3 Advanced Components. Claimed effects include complete vacuum resistance, substantial cold resistance when clothed, improved resistance to environmental toxins, and improved breathing, with reduced eating, talking, and beauty values and a constant −10 mood effect. The page is tagged only for 1.6 and requires Odyssey. Steam's generic removal/incompatibility banner gives no cause.
