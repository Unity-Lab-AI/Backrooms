## Source facts

The publisher describes an ore highlight that appears while the vanilla Mine designator is active. A configurable Shift-click can designate an entire ore vein. The publisher's comments clarify that the overlay does not appear with other mining tools such as Allow Tool's Strip Mine, and that vein selection expects a click on one cell or the displayed marker rather than a dragged multi-cell selection. The Workshop page lists Harmony as required.

Steam displays removed-item and “incompatible with RimWorld” notices. These are page notices only; they do not establish an in-game incompatibility. No license or publisher-linked source repository was visible.
