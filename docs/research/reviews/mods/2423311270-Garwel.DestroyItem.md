## Source facts

The publisher describes an Architect order that lets hauling pawns destroy items over time without returning resources or making filth. Multiple pawns can work on one item. Pawns may dislike destroying humanlike corpses or embryos. Wastepacks can emit toxic gas and pollute terrain; zero-hit-point items such as silver, steel, and plasteel can be destroyed immediately.
