# RimWorld mod review: TendYourself

- Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3476618866
- Workshop ID: `3476618866`; local package ID: `msd.tendyourself`
- Review date: 2026-09-27; local version not declared; local support declaration 1.4–1.6
- Local metadata declares no dependency, order, or conflict. No exact-source repository or license was verified.

## Verified source facts

The publisher says any humanlike downed pawn, including raiders, may self-tend before crawling away. The action is limited to bleeding wounds and requires consciousness and manipulation; the publisher describes lower-quality, slower tending and configurable medical-skill/restriction thresholds. The page says “multiplayer compatible” and that it was tested on 1.5; it does not establish RimWorld Together support. An author comment explains bruise-only injuries are ineligible because they are not bleeding. That comment is publisher information, not a reproduced result.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional recovery aid for combat and expeditions; not guaranteed recovery or secure custody.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-EXP; RR-THREAT; RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep first aid, evacuation, and crew-return rules available without self-tending. A hostile pawn recovering should remain a threat, not be treated as captured.
- Future check: bleeding versus non-bleeding, consciousness/manipulation, skill/medical restrictions, medicine use, tending quality/time, interruption, hostile return-to-combat, save/load, selected medical-care mods, and RWT pawn-state ownership.
