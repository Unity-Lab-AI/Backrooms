## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes fetch and pet-walking jobs that provide recreation and some animal skill. Non-colonist/guest interactions require two settings. The publisher recommends Hospitality Continued and says adding/removing while a pawn or animal is mid-job can produce a one-time load error that corrects after another save. The page declares LGPL; the exact version could not be verified because the linked license page was inaccessible during this review. The normal Workshop listing was available; a generic removal banner was not observed.

**Provisional disposition:** optional staff/pet recreation; no adapter or dependency. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-COMPAT`. Hospitality guests remain distinct from asynchronous RWT facility visits.
