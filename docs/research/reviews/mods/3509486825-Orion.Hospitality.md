## Verified source facts

- Hospitality adds in-world faction guests, lodging/joy, relations, recruitment, shops, comms invites, guest management and chore assistance.
- Its guest state is separate from a RimWorld Together visit by another player's company/facility. The publisher's Odyssey/gravship caveats are not RWT runtime findings.
- Do not copy code or licensed artwork into Rimrooms.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional:** optional native guest-economy content; no direct adapter planned until a specific use case warrants it.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-STA`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-MSN`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-MP`, `RR-COMPAT`; see the [feature map](../../../FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md).
- Do not use Hospitality as the system for cross-company visits or shared branch ownership. Keep its guests and store economy independent of Rimrooms company state.
