## Verified source facts

- The publisher describes locating oil reservoirs, drilling, crude/fuel pipes and tanks, a console-managed refinery, chemfuel power and material/medicine production, and spill incidents.
- The Workshop page supports RimWorld 1.6, was updated 2026-09-24, and says it is multiplayer ready. These are publisher statements, not tests of the selected multiplayer setup.
- The local package version and 1.6 support are recorded in the installed snapshot. Its lack of a declared Harmony dependency differs from the Workshop page and needs package-level reconciliation.
- Rimefeller overlaps with the optional Rimatomics and battery power systems. It does not document a Rimrooms gate API.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use its native industrial and power features as optional content; no Rimrooms adapter planned.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-GATE`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Do not make oil extraction, chemfuel, or this mod's material chain necessary for the gate, company economy, or first expedition. Keep an independent Core route.
