## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a broad building and retexture expansion for walls, doors, corners, and floors, with airtight tags on applicable structures and gravship halls. It warns that other mods retexturing vanilla walls/doors probably will not work and describes included door-clearance/perspective behavior. These are publisher statements, not profile test results.

**Provisional disposition:** optional architecture reference, no copied art/code or adapter. Its broad door/retexture scope makes it a priority interaction check. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-GATE`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-STYLE`, `RR-COMPAT`.
