## Verified source facts

- It can automatically designate tree stumps and blighted plants for cutting, and adds manual orders for stumps and fully grown trees/crops.
- The Workshop change notes record a 1.6 update and a later correction to queued jobs. The mod can be configured in-game.
- It changes ordinary plant-cutting work, not Rimrooms campaign data. No code or assets are copied.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional quality-of-life behaviour; no Rimrooms dependency or duplicate job system planned.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Let players keep their existing groundskeeping orders on facilities and outposts. Rimrooms should not override stump, blight, or crop designations.
