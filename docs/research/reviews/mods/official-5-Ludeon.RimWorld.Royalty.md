## Official scope and Rimrooms use

Ludeon describes the Empire, quests and honor, noble titles and throne rooms, permits such as troops/shuttles/labor, psycasts and Imperial equipment. These are optional avenues for company prestige, contracts, specialist roles or transport; they are not Rimrooms' authority for ranks, gate travel or payroll.

**Disposition:** optional DLC integration. Related features: `RR-DLC`, `RR-STA`, `RR-MSN`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-COMPAT`. All ranks, rewards and transport need a Core route.
