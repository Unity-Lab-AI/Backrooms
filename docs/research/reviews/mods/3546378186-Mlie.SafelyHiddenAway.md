## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says remote bases are raided less often, island travel affects timing, caravans/visitors arrive less often, and high wealth can cancel delays; timing is configurable. It says it works with older saves and mentions a raid-timer error on removal.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional world-event pacing; Rimrooms owns outpost threat schedules and cannot promise safety through this mod.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-OUT; RR-MSN; RR-THREAT; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** nearby versus remote sites, wealth settings, raid timers, caravan/visitor intervals, save/load/removal, and whether Rimrooms outposts map to affected world locations.

No threat scheduling or site behavior was tested.
