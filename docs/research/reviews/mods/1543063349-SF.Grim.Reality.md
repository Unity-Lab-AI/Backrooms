## Verified source facts

- The mod changes pawn mood and opinion rules, making routine discomfort matter less and serious loss, violence, and social relationships matter more. It also makes shared sleeping rooms more acceptable.
- Its local metadata only lists RimWorld 1.0–1.4. The Workshop page currently tags through 1.4 as well. Its presence in the selected 1.6 profile is not proof of 1.6 support.
- The Workshop page calls the work open source and gives a one-year permission-request note. Rimrooms will not copy its code, text, or assets.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** keep it in the selected profile record, but do not depend on its mood balance or promise 1.6 compatibility. Avoid reworking vanilla thought rules to imitate it.
- Related systems: `RR-STA`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- If Rimrooms adds staff thoughts, keep them isolated and test their load order and outcomes with this mod present and absent. The company campaign should not require the mod to explain staff behaviour.
