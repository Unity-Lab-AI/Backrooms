## Publisher and package evidence

The publisher describes wood-fired and chemfuel crematoria. Smithing unlocks the wood version; Biofuel Refining unlocks the chemfuel version. The page lists compatibility patches for Rim of Madness – Bones and Ashes. The publisher comments state that the 1.6 update added the chemfuel variant, a later patch added Bone China recipes, and an Ashes patch was added on 1 February (year not shown on the comment). The page advertises five change notes, but its separate changelog endpoint was not readable during this review, so those notes were not independently checked.

The installed 1.6 XML contains two 3×2 crematorium buildings using vanilla cremation bills. The wood version uses WoodLog; the chemfuel version uses Chemfuel and adds one industrial component to its recipe. Both variants have research prerequisites and patches that add their defs to the vanilla cremation bill list. Conditional patches add Bones/Ashes recipes when those optional packages are present. These local XML facts describe the installed copy; they do not establish that all optional patches work together in play.

Steam displays a community-removal notice and a generic “incompatible with RimWorld” notice on the publisher page. The page does not identify a specific technical cause or an explanation for removal. Treat these as page-state observations, not runtime findings.
