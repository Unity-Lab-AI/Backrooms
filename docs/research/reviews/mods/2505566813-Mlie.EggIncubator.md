## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a passive-cooling primitive incubator and an electricity-research industrial incubator with an optional speed setting. User comments about Alpha Animals eggs and humanoid eggs are not reproduced.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional animal/facility furnishing.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-STA; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** egg temperature, power loss, speed, hatch, and save/load; then selected modded eggs, animal management, and shared egg ownership only if co-op transfer is in scope.

No egg behavior or profile compatibility was tested.
