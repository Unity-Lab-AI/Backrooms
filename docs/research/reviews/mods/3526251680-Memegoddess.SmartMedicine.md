## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes medicine selection from pawn/patient inventories, optional stock-up, lower-tier medicine for nonurgent injuries, field tending, surgery medicine choice, and per-injury settings. Per-injury settings do not persist after loading, according to the page. It says to stop stock-up before removal; a later load may log an error. The publisher says it works with Pharmacist, which is absent from this inventory. Steam comments about prisoner surgery settings and field-tending errors are unverified reports.

**Provisional disposition:** optional medical-care QoL; no Rimrooms adapter. Rimrooms must retain its own care and staff recovery route. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
