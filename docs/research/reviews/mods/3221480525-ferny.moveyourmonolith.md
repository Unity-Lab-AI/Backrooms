## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher permits claiming/reinstalling the monolith and warns to move it before activation. Moving after activation may render it incorrectly, prevent attunement, or create another monolith quest. “Compatible with pretty much everything” is a publisher claim only.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional Anomaly facility-layout utility; it does not implement the Rimrooms machine gate, coordinates, or generated-space navigation.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** move before/after activation, attunement, quest uniqueness, map transfer and save/load; verify absence with no Anomaly. Test selected construction/map tools separately.

No profile behavior is certified.
