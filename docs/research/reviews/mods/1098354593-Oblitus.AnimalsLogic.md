## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher lists optional animal features including production information, medical alerts, configurable hauling/tameness/training, and predator hostility. Its source exposes persisted `hostile_predators` (default `true`); a Harmony postfix to `GenHostility.HostileTo` treats a predator hunting a faction pawn as hostile. The publisher says predator hunters become hostile to pawns and turrets. Its multiplayer claim points to a separate compatibility patch and does not establish RimWorld Together support.

The selected profile also has Turrets Shoot Hunting Predators (234). That publisher identifies Animals Logic as an overlapping/incompatible predator-targeting feature and documents the option to disable Animals Logic's predator-shooting feature while keeping its other features. This is a documented coexistence path, not a reproduced result. Turrets Shoot Hunting Predators also says it ceases to work with Combat Extended's altered target check and mods replacing vanilla turret `TryFindNewTarget`.

**Provisional disposition:** optional animal QoL; no Rimrooms adapter or dependency. If row 234 is retained, either rely on Animals Logic alone or disable `hostile_predators` before testing the second mod. Related features: `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
