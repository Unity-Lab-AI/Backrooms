## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes an orbital scanner that finds rich asteroid maps for selected resources, with a fuel, labor, and time cost. It is researched alongside the Long-Range Mineral Scanner and built in the Misc tab. A setting controls under-roof scanning and cell count. It mines `Mineable` definitions with `mineableThing` and `veinMineable`; custom `ModExtension` resources outside those patterns are excluded. Odyssey is required.

**Provisional disposition:** optional orbital mining/salvage reference with no direct integration. Odyssey is not required for the Core campaign; maintain a Rimrooms extraction fallback. Related features: `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-COMPAT`.
