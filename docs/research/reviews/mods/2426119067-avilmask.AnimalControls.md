## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes animal food restrictions, filters, crop/tree protection, handler-food settings, and defaults for joining/newborn animals. It reports a known issue with Replimat and Meal Printer, which are not in this 294-row inventory. The publisher says adding mid-save is supported but removing it can leave generally harmless errors or require redrafting. Workshop comments about handler food loads remain unverified reports.

**Provisional disposition:** optional animal-logistics QoL; no adapter or dependency. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-STA`, `RR-COMPAT`. Avoid duplicating its animal food-policy controls; do not make company logistics depend on it.
