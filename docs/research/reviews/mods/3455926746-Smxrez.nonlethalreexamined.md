# RimWorld mod review: Non-Lethal: Re-Examined

- Steam Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3455926746
- Workshop ID: `3455926746`; local package ID: `Smxrez.nonlethalreexamined`
- Review date: 2026-09-27; local version not declared; local support declaration 1.5–1.6
- Local metadata requires Vanilla Expanded - Non-Lethal (row 250) and loads after it. That dependency requires Harmony and Vanilla Expanded Framework. No exact-source repository or license for this add-on was verified; do not assume row 250's separate license statement covers it.
- The publisher explicitly says this add-on is incompatible with Combat Extended. CE is not selected in this inventory.

## Verified source facts

The publisher describes changes to the taser and rubber pistol, a rubber shotgun, a wall-mounted taser turret that can stun/down pawns (including in prisoner/drop-pod contexts), and a tear-gas launcher debuff. It says the add-on should be safe to add but could be dangerous to remove, and suggests Prisoners Should Fear Turrets as an escape-targeting companion and a lower death-on-down chance. Those are publisher recommendations, not reproduced results. A user comment says the rubber shotgun may destroy body parts or kill; this remains unverified. Row 250's broader CE statement does not supersede this add-on's explicit CE incompatibility.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional weapon/turret/custody aid; not assured non-lethality or capture.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-THREAT; RR-STA; RR-MSN; RR-COMPAT; RR-MP`.
- Keep Core security, capture, and escape response functional without these weapons. No Rimrooms threat should be balanced on a guaranteed stun.
- Future check: research/recipes, stun/down versus injury/death/body-part destruction, gas, wall turret and prisoner targets, row 177 turret behavior, capture rows 60/287, death-on-down settings, add/remove saves, and two-client RWT custody. Do not promise CE support for this add-on.

## Runtime evidence

No weapon, custody, full-profile, CE, or RWT test was run. Publisher claims and comments are not reproduced behavior.
