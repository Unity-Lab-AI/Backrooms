## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says the mechanoid can feed and play with children and fight fires; it uses Standard Mechtech research and a Large Gestator. Workshop comments report 1.6 feeding/play problems and a claimed partial fix, but those are community reports rather than established publisher support or reproduced results. The page credits XML, transpilers, 1.6 work, and artwork to multiple contributors.

**Provisional disposition:** optional Biotech automation/care-adjacent content; no Rimrooms dependency or bridge. Core-only staffing and care remain available without it. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-COMPAT`.
