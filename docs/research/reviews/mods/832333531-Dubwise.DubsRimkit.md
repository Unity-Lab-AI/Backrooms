## Verified source facts

- The mod adds refillable accessory kits. Medical kits provide self-tending, bed tending, target tending, and automatic use of stored medicine.
- Repair kits change construction/repair success and allow component salvage, with salvaged components repaired at a machining table.
- The Workshop page is tagged for RimWorld 1.6 but currently displays removal and compatibility notices without a stated technical reason.
- Workshop comments suggest possible interactions with Common Sense and Smart Medicine. These are user reports, not confirmed conflicts; both mods are in this profile.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use native staff-care and repair features as optional player tools; no Rimrooms adapter planned.
- Related systems: `RR-STA`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Do not make a particular kit, medicine tier, or component-recovery mechanic necessary for the first gate operation or basic injury recovery.

## Runtime evidence

- Not runtime tested. Test kit equip/refill/tend modes, repair and salvage, and the named Common Sense, Smart Medicine, Prison Labor, and RimWorld Together interactions on the selected profile. Verify pawn and item ownership across a visit or transfer before claiming multiplayer behavior.
