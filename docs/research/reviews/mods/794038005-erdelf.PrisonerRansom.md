## Verified source facts

- The publisher describes requesting silver for an imprisoned pawn through the comms console. Social skill, goodwill, requested amount, and settings can affect the outcome; a failed demand may lead to a raid.
- The local package includes code replacing/adding the ransom interaction. No Rimrooms ledger API is advertised.
- The Workshop page currently displays Steam removal and compatibility notices without a stated technical reason. The publisher's save-compatibility claim has not been reproduced.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use as optional native ransom behavior; do not duplicate its comms action or assume accepted ransom pays a Rimrooms company ledger.
- Related systems: `RR-STA`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-MSN`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Company fees and reimbursements must have a separate, auditable transaction route and work when this mod is absent.
