## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes tear gas, tasers, dart weapons, and a stun baton for incapacitating one or more targets; items are crafted after research. It claims Combat Extended compatibility and save-add compatibility, and says hostile pawns do not spawn with these weapons by default. The page credits graphics/XML and poison-gas code to other contributors. Workshop comments about gas masks/toxic resistance are not confirmed publisher support.

**Provisional disposition:** optional third-party nonlethal equipment; leave independent, with no Rimrooms data dependency. Keep a Core route for restraint and security. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
