## Official scope and Rimrooms use

Ludeon describes gravships, planetary travel, biomes/landmarks, orbital sites, space survival, wildlife and quests. Odyssey can add optional staging, logistics, exploration and late-game travel. The gate and Rimrooms coordinate/outpost system remain the campaign travel backbone when Odyssey is absent. Ludeon's 1.6 update notes also describe a vacuum-protective Empire prosthetic that requires both Odyssey and Royalty; such cross-DLC content remains an optional pair-specific route.

**Disposition:** optional DLC integration. Related features: `RR-DLC`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-MSN`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-COMPAT`.
