## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes gradually synchronizing crops in growing zones toward simultaneous maturity, with settings for synchronization amount and interval.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional harvest-scheduling aid; don't assume fixed yields/timing or make supply contracts depend on it.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-OUT; RR-ECO; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** growing-zone behavior/settings, crop timing, harvest/stockpile capacity, ordinary versus hydroponic crops, and food delivery/hauling. Keep no-mod supply routes.
