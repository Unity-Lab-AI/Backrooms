## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes settings to multiply existing armor toughness and make armor penetration matter more. It says calculations apply to humanlike pawns, animals, mechanoids and modded horrors using vanilla armor/weapon values. The page characterizes Combat Extended compatibility as theoretical and possibly requiring settings changes; this is not a tested result.

**Provisional disposition:** optional combat-balance settings; do not make it own Rimrooms injury state. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.

## Runtime follow-up

No runtime test was run. Check armor/penetration combinations, resulting injuries and body parts, melee/ranged damage and armored threats. Do not include Combat Extended in support without separate evidence.
