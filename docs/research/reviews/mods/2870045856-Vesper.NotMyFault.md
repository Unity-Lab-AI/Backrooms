## Source facts and Rimrooms use

Publisher says it removes the −5 faction-relation penalty when visitors die on the player's tile from events outside player control; intentional harm still incurs penalties. The page acknowledges possible exploits and claims broad compatibility because it changes the vanilla value.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional relationship-balance patch; not Rimrooms reputation or contract-failure authority.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-OUT; RR-ECO; RR-STA; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** distinguish uncontrolled deaths from intentional harm, then test factions, guests/traders, contracts, save/load. Multiplayer behavior remains untested.

Rimrooms company reputation remains independent.
