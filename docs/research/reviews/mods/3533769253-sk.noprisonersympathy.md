## Verified source facts

- The publisher describes reducing or removing mood penalties for prisoner-related actions, including killing, harvesting, banishing, butchering, and enslaving. Settings control some effects such as goodwill loss.
- The source uses Harmony patches to alter the game's witnessed-action thoughts and related prisoner/precept outcomes. No supported Rimrooms extension API is advertised.
- The publisher says this is a fresh C# rewrite rather than a continuation of the older Workshop item 2167490674.
- The page says it is compatible with War Crimes Expanded 2 and Beat Your Prisoners (Continued); that is a publisher statement, not a reproduced profile result.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** configuration only; no Rimrooms integration planned.
- Related systems: `RR-STA`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Custody, interviews, and investigation must remain playable without this mod and must not assume that every company action is mood-neutral.
