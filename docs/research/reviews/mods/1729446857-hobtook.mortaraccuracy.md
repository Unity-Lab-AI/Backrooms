## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes configurable accuracy, target leading, weather/time and skill effects, radius previews, and options affecting cover misses. It says the behavior can apply to mortar-like artillery, including modded artillery. A generic multiplayer reference does not establish RWT support.

**Provisional disposition:** optional defense-balance feature; no Rimrooms dependency. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`. Keep Combat Extended outside the supported profile.
