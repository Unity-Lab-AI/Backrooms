## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes a continuation/rework adding animal equivalents for many vanilla prosthetics and bionics, with research and animal-category settings. XML Extensions can bypass some category restrictions. The page lists selected compatibility integrations, says Alpha Animals is unsupported, and notes removal can make installed or stored parts disappear. The Steam page has a generic removed/incompatible banner without a stated reason. The source repository is public but no license was verified; public availability does not establish reuse permission.

**Provisional disposition:** optional animal medical/technology content; no Rimrooms dependency or adapter. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-COMPAT`. Keep an animal-care route that does not require this mod, and keep case/evidence records in Rimrooms-owned data.
