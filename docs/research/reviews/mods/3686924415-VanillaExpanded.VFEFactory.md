## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes bill-driven factory targets, overclocking/power/heat, conveyors and filtered bulk production. It says pawns can still use factory hoppers without conveyors, belts can spoil items, and performance cost grows with belt count. The page says no DLC is required, with additional functionality if Biotech/Odyssey is active. Change notes mention an astrofuel recipe and gravship building fix; these are not proof of compatibility with the selected gravship chapters. A belt-item loss report during launch remains unverified.

**Provisional disposition:** major optional production expansion. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-DLC`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-COMPAT`. Keep Core-only production and economy independent of it.
