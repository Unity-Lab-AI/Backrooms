## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The page describes late-game intercontinental VTOLs and an airship drawing cargo from stockpile zones. It warns about weight, seats, turret fuel use, and retaining fuel for a return trip. Its page has an inconsistency: one section says it works with Transport Shuttle Standalone (Continued), while another warns against Transport Shuttle; both it and SRTS Expanded are named in conflict notes. It says vanilla 1.6 has similar items and future support will end, while this local package declares 1.6. Steam displays a generic removal notice.
