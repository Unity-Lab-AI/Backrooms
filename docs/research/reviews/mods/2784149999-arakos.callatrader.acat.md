## Verified source facts

- The publisher describes requesting an orbital trader at a comms console, with configurable cost, delay, cooldown, and letter timeout. The page cautions against relying on later game versions.
- The profile also contains `Call Trade Ships (Continued)`, row 17, which provides another orbital-trader request path.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use the native trader call as an optional feature; do not couple Rimrooms contracts or company finances to it.
- Related systems: `RR-ECO`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep scheduled shipments and company procurement on their own contract/ledger path.
