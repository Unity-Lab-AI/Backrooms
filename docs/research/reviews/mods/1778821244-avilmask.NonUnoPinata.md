## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes pawns retaining carried weapons/inventory when downed or killed, with items extracted when stripped. Cremation/butchering auto-strip behavior is configurable, and players can choose what is removed from corpses, downed pawns, or prisoners. Public source visibility does not establish reuse permission.

**Provisional disposition:** optional pawn/corpse inventory behavior; do not treat corpse contents as Rimrooms' evidence ledger. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-MP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
