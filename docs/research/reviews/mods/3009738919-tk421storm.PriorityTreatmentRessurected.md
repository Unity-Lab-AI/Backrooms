## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says doctors with manual Doctor priority 1 prioritize emergency tending over other work and may skip their own needs. Settings cover sickness urgency, waking doctors, emergency feeding, and restricting injured surgeons. The author says Wake Up is safe but can be redundant when both wake options are active. The page identifies an ongoing issue with a differently named Smart Medicine mod, where medicine selection can produce dropped-medicine/job loops; Smart Medicine - Continued (209) is selected, but the relationship is a test lead, not a confirmed conflict. A broad author-reported test set does not certify this profile.

**Provisional disposition:** optional emergency-care QoL, not a Rimrooms medical system. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
