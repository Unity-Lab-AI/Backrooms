## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes large name pools, nicknames, romanized/international options, and configurable name/nickname probabilities. User comments report wrong-gender names and custom-faction exceptions in 1.6; these are unverified reports.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional generated-pawn naming; not lore names or identity keys.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-STYLE; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** language settings, international options, pawn/faction generation, duplicates, custom-faction generation, gender/name combinations, and save/load; then visitor names and RWT transfers. Keep stable internal IDs separate from names.

Verify name-data provenance before redistribution. No assets or data are bundled in Rimrooms.
