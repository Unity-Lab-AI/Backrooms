## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes an animal-wearable vacuum-survival belt unlocked by Basic Gravtech research and crafted at a machining table. It requires both Odyssey and Animal Apparel: Framework. The author says it can be worn with Basic Armor but not at the same time as a shield belt or jump pack.

**Provisional disposition:** optional Odyssey-gated animal space gear; never a Core-only, gate, or expedition requirement. Related features: `RR-DLC`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
