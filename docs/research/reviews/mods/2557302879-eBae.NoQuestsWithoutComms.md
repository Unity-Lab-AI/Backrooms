## Verified source facts

- The publisher says most quests are held until a working comms device or substitute exists. A setting allows some local quest events to appear without comms.
- The author says the Harmony behavior applies to quests fired through `IncidentWorker_GiveQuest`, not arbitrary incidents or custom quest generators, and notes the 1.6 update was not extensively tested.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** configuration only; do not route Rimrooms' own contracts or objectives through this optional quest filter.
- Related systems: `RR-MSN`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep the Core campaign's first objectives and local incidents available before the player builds comms equipment.

## Runtime evidence

- Not runtime tested. Compare quest availability before and after comms construction, then with Go Explore! and More Faction Interaction. Confirm Rimrooms objectives remain available in Core-only and with this filter enabled.
