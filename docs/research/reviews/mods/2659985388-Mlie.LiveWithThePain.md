## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes permanent-pain injuries becoming less painful over time: 75% after five days, 50% after a quadrum, and 25% after a year, with configurable stages and optional weather/temperature/waking modifiers. There is an option to affect only controllable pawns.

**Provisional disposition:** optional injury-recovery tuning, no adapter or dependency. Keep expedition recovery understandable without its pain progression. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
