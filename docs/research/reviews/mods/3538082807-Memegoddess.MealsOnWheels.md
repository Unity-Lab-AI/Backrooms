## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says colonists may take meals from animals or other pawns when other food is unavailable, and claims no save impact/no conflict with Smarter Food Selection. A Workshop comment reports repeated failed jobs with Pick Up And Haul; the continued-mod author says the issue was not fixed at that time. This remains a report, not a reproduced behavior or confirmed fix status.

**Provisional disposition:** optional meal-access convenience; do not rely on it for expedition/outpost ration accounting. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
