## Verified source facts

- The stated feature is a player action for prioritizing research. The public code includes a float-menu patch for the research-priority action; it is not a replacement research tree or a Rimrooms technology API.
- A publisher comment dated 2026-08-04 says to load this mod before Rimatomics and other mods that add research benches. The selected profile already orders this mod at row 76 and Rimatomics at row 83.
- A Workshop discussion includes a user report that Rimatomics' separate research table stopped accepting research priority. That report is not a reproduced compatibility finding, but it makes the pair a required test.
- The public repository page does not show a reuse license, and the Workshop page disclaims ownership of the earlier mod. Do not copy its files into Rimrooms.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** configuration-only; preserve its earlier-than-Rimatomics load position and do not build a Rimrooms dependency on its menu action or research internals.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-GATE`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- The Rimrooms research plan needs its own queue/progress contract and must remain usable when this optional work-priority mod is absent.
