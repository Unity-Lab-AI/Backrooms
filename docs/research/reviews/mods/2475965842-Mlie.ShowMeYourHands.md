# RimWorld mod review: Show Me Your Hands

- Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2475965842
- Source: https://github.com/emipa606/ShowMeYourHands
- Row 204; package `Mlie.ShowMeYourHands`; local version 1.6.10; support 1.1–1.6; Harmony required and loaded before it.
- Local metadata conflicts with `Mlie.ClutterWeaponHands` (not selected) and loads after an uninstalled RIMMSqol package. Continued source repo lists MIT.
- Reviewed 2026-09-27; no runtime test.

## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes hand placement on weapons while drafted, dynamic placement from weapon graphics, per-weapon settings, and exportable native-support settings. It lists Simple Sidearms, RunAndGun, and Dual Wield support and says it is verified with Combat Extended; those claims do not establish this profile's behavior.

- **Provisional disposition:** visual presentation only; don't copy textures or make equipment readability depend on it.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STYLE; RR-THREAT; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** selected Simple Sidearms and Dual Wield; weapon changes, animation, carrying, missing/artificial limbs, nonhuman pawns; verify the uninstalled conflicting mod remains absent.

No visual/profile result was tested.
