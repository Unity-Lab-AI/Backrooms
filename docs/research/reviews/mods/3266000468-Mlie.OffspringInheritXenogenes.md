## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says xenogenes and archite genes can pass to offspring, with configurable inheritance chances. Conception determines pregnancy genes. It says the mod can be added/removed from existing saves.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional Biotech pawn-genetics behavior; no staffing/recruitment dependency.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** Biotech present/absent, conception, settings changes, save/load, and interactions with Better Gene Inheritance (51), Force Xenogerm Implantation (94), Inject Genes (112), and Integrated Genes (114). Keep Rimrooms staff progression independent.

No combined gene behavior or RWT transfer was tested.
