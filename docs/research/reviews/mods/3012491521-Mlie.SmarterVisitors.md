## Verified source facts

The publisher describes delayed visitor departures when a scheduled departure coincides with map danger or medical rest; an optional Biotech UV-sensitivity gene can also delay departure during daytime. Settings can add longer-stay mood effects and food rations. A Visitor spot is included. The page says this affects time-triggered departures, not departures caused by hostility/temperature or manual dismissal, and describes compatibility with Hospitality. The local profile contains Hospitality, but its guest lifecycle is distinct from an offline RimWorld Together facility visit.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional visitor-behavior complement; no direct adapter.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-STA; RR-MSN; RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep company visits and the company ledger independent of Hospitality/visitor pawn behavior. Biotech-specific UV behavior must disappear cleanly without Biotech.
- Future check: visitor departure timers under danger, injury/rest, optional UV sensitivity with Biotech present/absent, rations and mood, Visitor spot/pathing, manual dismissal, and separate RWT offline visits.
