## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says idle pawns gain/lose joy differently so they do more recreation; boredom and the idle-pawn notification remain. The page says RocketMan can cause a recreation loop and describes reports involving Need Bar Overflow. The selected profile includes Need Bar Overflow (row 142); those reports are not reproduced. The page says the mod was commissioned from Taranchuk and that the current publisher's compatibility capacity is limited.

**Provisional disposition:** optional recreation behavior; no adapter or dependency. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-COMPAT`. Do not rely on it to meet staff needs or satisfy progression.
