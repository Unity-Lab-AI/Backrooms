## Source facts and Rimrooms use

Publisher describes pen filters for age, pregnancy, sterilization, pack cargo, tending, malnutrition, slaughter designation, harvestable resources, injuries, and names. One marker combines filters with AND; multiple markers combine with OR. For removal, the publisher says allow every filter, save, then remove the mod.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional animal-management QoL.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-STA; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** mixed pens, marker movement, AND/OR behavior, “harvestable resources” with row135, save/load, and the publisher's removal steps. No Rimrooms animal workflow depends on it.

No source or runtime compatibility is claimed.
