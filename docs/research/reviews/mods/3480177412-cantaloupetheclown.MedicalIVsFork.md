## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes IV equipment, Biotech hemogen support (Biotech is not required), bloodstem refining, grouped research, architect categories, and Dubs Bad Hygiene support. The author says IVs were tested with ordinary, animal, and hospital beds; this is a publisher claim, not this profile's result. The page says it lacks built-in support for other mods' painkillers/antibiotics; a user report about a Glitterworld painkiller is unverified.

**Provisional disposition:** optional native medical equipment; no Rimrooms adapter. Keep staff treatment usable without it. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-FAC`, `RR-COMPAT`.
