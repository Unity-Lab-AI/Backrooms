## Source facts and Rimrooms use

Publisher describes patches that add milking/shearing for additional animals, generic milk, distinct wool types, and reduced output for harder-to-milk animals. Optional Odyssey animal patches are described. The Regrowth: Extinct Animals table is awaiting that package's 1.6 update.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional animal-resource variety; not a Rimrooms economy requirement.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-STA; RR-ECO; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** jobs, yields/duplication, save/load, vanilla and optional Odyssey animals, later supported animal packs, and row133's harvest-ready pen filter.

No compatibility or asset reuse is established.
