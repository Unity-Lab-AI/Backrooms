## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes pawns collecting multiple items into their inventory before hauling to stockpiles, plus an order to keep an item in inventory. The page lists limitations for guests and prisoners and says unloading is not perfect.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional hauling QoL; Rimrooms owns cargo records and must retain vanilla hauling.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-EXP; RR-OUT; RR-MP; RR-COMPAT`.
- Row 107 says Haul to Stack no longer acts with this mod on newer game versions; row 125 has a user report/author response about repeated failed food jobs. Neither is a reproduced current result. Test inventory/unload, corpses, categories, priorities, caravan/outpost provisioning, both named pairs, item loss/duplication, save/load, and RWT transfers.

No selected-profile or multiplayer support is established.
