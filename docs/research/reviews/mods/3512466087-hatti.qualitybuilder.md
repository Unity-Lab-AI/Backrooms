## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says the mod sets minimum construction quality and deconstructs/replaces items below that threshold. It describes 1.6 fixes for Ideology furniture and “unforbid on reload.”

- **Provisional disposition:** optional construction QoL; do not require quality thresholds or promise resource-neutral replacement.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-FAC; RR-UI; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** below-threshold furniture/buildings, deconstruction and resource use, work assignment, overlapping objects, reload behavior, optional Ideology furnishings, and Core-only fallback.

No quality/rebuild compatibility is established.
