## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says the storefront can work with vanilla caravan visitors or Hospitality guests; Cash Register is the required dependency. A staffed shop lists nearby-area goods, while seller social skill affects mood and negotiated price; visitors may buy above their personal budget from faction funds. With Gastronomy, the publisher says to close one mode or use separate cash registers to avoid staff seeking the wrong job.

**Provisional disposition:** optional business-scenario flavor only; not required for Rimrooms' company economy or selling loop. No reuse of its assets/code. Related features: `RR-SCEN`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-STA`, `RR-MP`, `RR-COMPAT`.
