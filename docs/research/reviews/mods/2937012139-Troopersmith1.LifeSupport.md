- Workshop ID: `2937012139`; local package ID: `Troopersmith1.LifeSupport`

- Local metadata requires Harmony and Death Rattle Continued (`Troopersmith1.DeathRattle`, row 71), and loads after both. The selected Death Rattle package is active.
- **Version/license boundary:** the Workshop page is Cat2002's updated continuation and points to a Troopersmith1 repository declaring MIT, but the repository currently exposes only older 1.1–1.3 trees/releases. The MIT repository does not establish licensing for the exact 1.6 Workshop payload, continuation changes, or every asset. Selected-fork rights remain unresolved.

## Verified source facts

The publisher says building life-support links to beds and can prevent death caused by loss of internal organs other than the brain; disconnecting/removing a bed with missing organs causes immediate death. It does not prevent bleedout or overdose. It requires Vitals Monitor and Life Support research, and the page says ordinary beds need no patch and Death Rattle works alongside it. Combat Extended is described as untested. User comments report a possible gravship medical-bed connection/UI issue; this is not reproduced. The page's generic removal banner does not state a reason.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** optional medical stabilization system; not environmental air, gate duration, or general crew survival.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-FAC; RR-EXP; RR-COMPAT; RR-SPACEFLIGHT`.
- Keep treatment and expedition recovery valid without the mod; do not promise its support for selected gravship beds or copy the selected fork.
- Future check: connected bed and power loss/removal, missing brain versus other organs, bleeding/overdose, bed types, Death Rattle, QEE Enhanced, WCE/other surgery mods, gravship medbay, save/load, and RWT pawn injury/custody ownership.
