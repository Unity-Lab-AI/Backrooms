## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes configurable inheritance of xenogenes, archite genes, and secondary-parent genes; shared parental genes are included in selection logic, and metabolism-cost genes may be removed as needed. It adds inheritance-control genes and attempts named/hybrid xenotype assignment. Publisher claims compatibility with Vanilla Races Expanded: Highmate genes; this is not profile evidence.

- **Provisional disposition:** optional Biotech offspring-genetics feature; no staff or recruitment dependency.
- **Feature mapping:** `RR-STA; RR-DLC; RR-COMPAT`.
- **Checks:** controlled parents, shared genes, metabolism, prerequisites, and generated/runtime xenotypes; save/load offspring; selected gene-pack/trade mods and RWT pawn transfer. Keep the Core staff loop independent.
