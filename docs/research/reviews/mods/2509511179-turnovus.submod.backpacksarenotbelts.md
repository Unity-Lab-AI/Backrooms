## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher describes moving packs from the waist layer to torso or separate equipment layers so packs can be worn with belts. Listed targets include Core smokepop packs; Royalty jump/low-shield packs; Biotech tox/control/bandwidth packs; and selected Vanilla Weapons Expanded, Rimmu-Nation, and Industrial Armory items. Settings include nudist handling and optional tactical-layer changes; some require restart. A current author comment says the VGE oxygen pack is supported. The selected profile also has Vanilla Apparel Expanded and Accessories; community equipment reports remain unverified.

**Provisional disposition:** optional equipment-layer patch, no Rimrooms adapter. Related features: `RR-STA`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-SPACEFLIGHT`, `RR-DLC`, `RR-COMPAT`. DLC remain optional; the listed items do not make Royalty or Biotech mandatory.
