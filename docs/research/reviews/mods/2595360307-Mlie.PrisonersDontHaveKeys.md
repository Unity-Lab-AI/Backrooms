## Verified source facts

- The publisher says prisoners must bash doors during a prison break, with settings for their own cell door and conditional rules for slaves or Anomaly entities.
- The page also describes support for Doors Expanded. Local 1.6 files include a Doors Expanded patch.
- The same door-access method is touched by Prison Commons. This is a confirmed shared patch point, not a confirmed conflict.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use optional native/config behavior; no Rimrooms door patch planned.
- Related systems: `RR-FAC`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Keep containment and prisoner movement understandable with ordinary doors and a vanilla fallback; do not use this setting as the only route for containing a threat.
