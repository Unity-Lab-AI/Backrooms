## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The page calls this an independent port/update of LWM's Deep Storage to ASF, with added or reworked storage objects and textures. Its instructions for replacing the original LWM mod do not automatically apply to this separate port. It says it should work with other storage mods. A user reports chunks disappearing after gravship takeoff; the ASF developer says the issue was not reproducible in isolation. This remains unverified.

**Provisional disposition:** optional installed storage/no touch. Retain vanilla storage as fallback. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-EVD`, `RR-ECO`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
