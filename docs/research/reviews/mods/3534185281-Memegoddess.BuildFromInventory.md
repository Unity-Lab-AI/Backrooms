## Source facts and Rimrooms use

The publisher says construction can use items from colonist or pack-animal inventories. Its retained original description says the builder's inventory is tried first, then ordinary resources, then other pawns' inventories, and construction may begin before all required materials are on hand. A comment reports an error with a differently named “While You're Up” mod; the author says the report concerns a shared patch target. The selected profile's “While You Are Nearby” is a different mod.

**Provisional disposition:** optional construction aid; no Rimrooms dependency. Related features: `RR-FAC`, `RR-EXP`, `RR-OUT`, `RR-MP`, `RR-COMPAT`. Rimrooms must make material ownership and consumption explicit.
