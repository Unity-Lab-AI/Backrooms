## Verified source facts

- The mod adds a `Capture` work type and a designation for downed non-faction pawns, so assigned workers can carry them to a prisoner bed.
- The public workgiver extends RimWorld's `WorkGiver_RescueDowned`, checks for the mod's designation, rejects nearby-danger cases, and looks for a prisoner bed before creating the vanilla `Capture` job. It automates the capture task; it does not add interview, evidence, interrogation, or long-term custody case management.
- The repository displays an MIT license. This review does not copy its code, textures, or other files into Rimrooms.
- `Prison Labor` is also in the selected profile at row 288. Their shared pawn/work/prisoner area is a test lead, not a confirmed incompatibility.

## Rimrooms integration decision

- **Provisional disposition:** use the native capture-work behavior as an optional convenience; keep vanilla capture, prisoner, and custody actions available without it. Rimrooms' investigation/case flow must not depend on this work type.
- Related systems: `RR-STA`, `RR-THREAT`, `RR-COMPAT`.
- Preserve a visible recovery path when no suitable prisoner bed exists or assigned workers cannot reach a target.
