# Gravship, vehicles, cargo, and orbital profile interactions

**Checked:** 2026-09-27
**Scope:** selected families in the 294-mod profile that may affect Odyssey gravships, vehicles, cargo, wrecks, trade, or space events. This is source research and a test plan, not compatibility evidence.

## Design boundary

The Backrooms gate, generated coordinates, room graph, and company progression stay native to Rimrooms. Vanilla Gravship Expanded (VGE) Chapters 1 and 2 are optional late-game Odyssey integrations for logistics, orbital work, and defense. The campaign must remain playable without Odyssey, VEF, either VGE chapter, or vehicle mods.

Do not copy or repackage other mods' code, art, text, or definitions. VGE Chapters 1 and 2, Vanilla Vehicles Expanded content, and ReBuild identify CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 terms; installed use is not permission to adapt their files. The Vehicle Framework repository identifies MIT for its own code, but that does not cover dependent mods. Unknown terms stay unknown until the exact publisher record is reviewed.

## Test sequence and evidence still needed

1. Record Core, DLC, Harmony, VEF, Odyssey, VGE versions, order, settings, logs, and a clean save.
2. Test Core + Harmony + VEF + Odyssey + both VGE chapters first. Check gravship build, crew, air, power, fuel, heat, launch, orbit, landing, and save/reload.
3. Add door/airtight/building candidates one at a time, then together. Record the exact symptom and remove one candidate at a time to isolate any reproduced interaction.
4. Add storage/cargo/mass mods and check items, chunks, placed buildings, and counts through launch, rotation, landing, and reload.
5. Add vehicles/wrecks, then trade/event families. Check travel, cargo and crew, map exits, costs, trader arrivals, landing spots, incident scope, and saves.
6. Run the full ordered 294 profile only after the family checks. Then test ordinary RWT visits, trade/aid, and reconnect separately from vehicle/gravship travel. Keep branch-owned company state local.

Every runtime result is still **Not run** for Rimrooms. Preserve failures and unknown license/version facts in the compatibility report. The full 294 profile remains a research and test target; none of these optional mods is a required dependency.

The Backrooms gate does not depend on VGE or vehicles.
