# Kane Pixels Backrooms continuity and story map

**Purpose:** organize the project's primary Backrooms reference into a traceable lore and story record before any Kane-specific content is designed. This is a research map, not a canon summary. A row becomes a source-backed entry only when its linked review record contains direct, timestamped observations and clearly separates stated facts from interpretation.

## Scope and evidence rules

- The project review set is the official **The Backrooms** playlist published by the verified Kane Pixels channel. The playlist page currently lists 23 uploads; this playlist order is a retrieval order, **not** an asserted in-universe chronology. Recheck the playlist before source-specific production.
- Review the actual video and any official captions/transcript available on that upload. Record when captions are unavailable. A missing transcript must stay missing; do not replace it with fan recollection or search snippets.
- Keep **source fact**, **interpretation**, **new gameplay idea**, and **unknown** easy to tell apart. A title, thumbnail, comment, or end-screen link alone is not enough to establish a plot event or character identity.
- Add timestamps for a few important beats or clues when useful. Keep upload date and any in-story date separate when both matter.
- Do not assume the Kane series and A24 feature share one continuity unless an official source establishes that relationship. Keep A24 review in its separate file.
- Use the source to ground the story and its atmosphere, then adapt it indirectly for RimWorld. Keep the game's characters, rooms, props, threats, mission order, and management rules original. Label source facts, interpretation, and new gameplay ideas clearly.

- Who works with the Backrooms, and what do they want?
- What do we learn about the machine, experiments, strange spaces, threats, and missing people?
- Which later uploads clarify an earlier story beat, and which links are still guesses?
- What makes good material for a RimWorld scenario, quest, expedition, facility, or research unlock?
