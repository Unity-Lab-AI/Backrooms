# Kane Pixels Backrooms continuity and story map

**Purpose:** keep a readable story notebook for the project's primary Backrooms reference. It should capture the people, organization, memorable spaces, strange events, and story ideas that can inspire RimWorld scenarios. It is not a shot-by-shot catalogue or a technical breakdown.

## Scope and evidence rules

- The review set is the official **The Backrooms** playlist from Kane Pixels. Its 23 entries are a convenient viewing list, not a confirmed story order. Check the playlist again before production.
- Watch each video and note the important story beats. Use a couple of timestamps only when they make a moment easy to find; do not catalogue every shot or turn each review into a technical report.
- Make it clear what the video states, what we infer, what we still do not know, and what we invent for the game. Do not use fan recollection as a substitute for what the source shows.
- Do not assume the Kane series and A24 feature share one continuity unless an official source establishes that relationship. Keep A24 review in its separate file.
- Use the source to ground the story and its atmosphere, then adapt it indirectly for RimWorld. Keep the game's characters, rooms, props, threats, mission order, and management rules original. Label source facts, interpretation, and new gameplay ideas clearly.

## Required continuity record for each upload

Use [`reviews/templates/kane-video-review-template.md`](reviews/templates/kane-video-review-template.md). Keep each note short and readable: what happens, who matters, what the place feels like, what remains mysterious, and one or two ideas for a scenario or quest. Label incomplete viewing honestly and separate source material from our game ideas.

## Cross-upload questions to answer from evidence

These are review prompts, **not predetermined lore conclusions**:

- Who works with the Backrooms, and what do they want?
- What do we learn about the machine, experiments, strange spaces, threats, and missing people?
- Which later uploads clarify an earlier story beat, and which links are still guesses?
- What makes good material for a RimWorld scenario, quest, expedition, facility, or research unlock?

## Synthesis and implementation handoff

Once every upload is reviewed, write a clear series summary, separate story order from playlist order, note the major characters/organizations/locations/threats, and outline a few story arcs. Tie the useful inspiration to the matching `RR-*` feature IDs and mark the rest as our own game design. Keep the A24 feature review separate until the official sources confirm a connection. Only mark the source-review gate complete after all uploads and the feature have been covered.
