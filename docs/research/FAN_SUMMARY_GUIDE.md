## Series overview recorded from the fan guide

The community series guide frames the story as beginning with a filmmaker's unsettling encounter in the yellow rooms, then following Async's attempts to study the opening and send people into the space. Its broad through-line is institutional curiosity meeting a place that does not behave like an ordinary building, with expeditions and disappearances raising the stakes. This is a fan-made overview, not a verified scene-by-scene account; concise notes for the 23 indexed uploads are recorded in [`KANE_PIXELS_FAN_CLIFF_NOTES.md`](KANE_PIXELS_FAN_CLIFF_NOTES.md).

The guide reports 19 main episodes and 25 total when hidden or supplemental entries are included, while the official Kane playlist index used by this project has 23 entries. Of the six hidden entries listed by the wiki, four appear in the local playlist index and two (`Faultline.mov` and `Simpsons`) do not. Keep the project index at 23 and the latter two in the supplemental notes; do not silently fold them into its index.

## A24 feature first-pass fan synopsis

The fan-maintained film page summarizes a furniture-showroom owner, Clark, finding a strange opening below the store. The spaces beyond it echo recognizable interiors but repeat and warp them. Staff go in and fail to return, and Mary later enters while searching for Clark. The page describes rooms shaped by personal memories and a later Async encounter. These are paraphrased fan-summary claims, not official synopsis details; use them as story leads and leave exact room rules and the ending's meaning open.

For RimWorld, the usable story cues are an ordinary workplace becoming the threshold, missing staff creating a case the company must manage, familiar spaces returning in distorted forms, and evidence or personal history helping explain a location. The store start, staff, company choices, and gameplay systems remain original design. The official [A24 synopsis](https://a24films.com/films/backrooms) independently confirms only the strange basement doorway in a furniture showroom. Keep this film note separate from the Kane series.
