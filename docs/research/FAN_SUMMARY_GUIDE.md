## Series overview recorded from the fan guide

The community series guide frames the story as beginning with a filmmaker's unsettling encounter in the yellow rooms, then following Async's attempts to study the opening and send people into the space. Its broad through-line is institutional curiosity meeting a place that does not behave like an ordinary building, with expeditions and disappearances raising the stakes. This is a fan-made overview, not a verified scene-by-scene account; concise notes for the 23 indexed uploads are recorded in [`KANE_PIXELS_FAN_CLIFF_NOTES.md`](KANE_PIXELS_FAN_CLIFF_NOTES.md).

Fan-wiki episode counters are community-maintained and have changed or conflicted with one another; the series page's current total is not the same as this project's captured official playlist. The project index preserves the 23 entries present in Kane's playlist on 2026-09-27, including **Everything Must Go**. Keep any fan-reported supplemental clips separately identified, and recheck the official playlist when source-specific content production starts.

## A24 feature first-pass fan synopsis

The fan-maintained film page summarizes a furniture-showroom owner, Clark, finding a strange opening below the store. The spaces beyond it echo recognizable interiors but repeat and warp them. Staff go in and fail to return, and Mary later enters while searching for Clark. The page describes rooms shaped by personal memories and a later Async encounter. These are paraphrased fan-summary claims, not official synopsis details; use them as story leads and leave exact room rules and the ending's meaning open.

For RimWorld, the usable story cues are an ordinary workplace becoming the threshold, missing staff creating a case the company must manage, familiar spaces returning in distorted forms, and evidence or personal history helping explain a location. The store start, staff, company choices, and gameplay systems remain original design. The official [A24 synopsis](https://a24films.com/films/backrooms) confirms the strange doorway in a furniture-showroom basement. A24's [production notes](https://praesens.com/praesens-pro-presse/katalog/backrooms/regional/de/backrooms-press-kit-dch/) place the feature in an Async story branch and say the organization has studied the Complex since an accidental 1989 opening during the web-series events. The fan plot outline remains secondary; track it in the separate feature note and do not treat fan interpretation as a confirmed story rule.
