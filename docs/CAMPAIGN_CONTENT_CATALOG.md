# Rimrooms - Async Industries: campaign content catalog

**Status:** pre-code design catalog, version 0.1. This is a player-facing content map, not a complete balance sheet, production asset list, implementation specification, or runtime result. All mechanics proposed here are **original Rimrooms design**. References to Kane Pixels or A24 are **indirect source cues** only; fan summaries are interpretations, not canon or proof of how a game mechanic must work.

## Campaign promise

Turn a precarious research company into a network that can enter, map, revisit, study, trade from, and build within unstable spaces. Every expedition begins with a reason to go, a crew and equipment plan, a known return method, and a choice about what is worth the risk. The company grows through records it can verify, people it can bring home, spaces it can secure, and technology it can afford to maintain.

The Backrooms are an expanding chain of finite, persistent destinations—not one endless map that must remain loaded. The company learns only what it has observed or acquired. A strange room may be dangerous, useful, rentable, salvageable, or all of those at once; no new discovery is automatically safe or profitable.

**Settled boundaries:** follow [Gate 0 decisions](GATE_0_DECISIONS.md): RimWorld 1.6; Core-only solo play; all five DLC optional; only Core plus Harmony/RimWorld Together required for the co-op route; all other entries in the 294-mod profile are optional. RWT cooperation is across separate company branches, without live shared-map control. Item and dossier exchange, visits, and any shared research remain conditional on the exact tested RWT route. The two Vanilla Gravship Expanded chapters are optional late-game integrations. Keep the exact displayed title `Rimrooms - Async Industries` and publisher metadata `Operator`.

- **Indirect Kane Pixels source cues:** institutional investigation, the engineered threshold, recorded tests and incomplete reports, missing crews, and familiar interiors that stop behaving as expected. The [fan cliff notes](research/KANE_PIXELS_FAN_CLIFF_NOTES.md) are a secondary story index; they do not establish canonical game rules.
- **Indirect A24 source cue:** the official feature synopsis describes a strange doorway in a furniture showroom's basement. The game’s store opening, staff, incidents, and outcomes are original design; see the separate [feature story note](research/reviews/a24-feature/feature-review.md).
- **Original design:** all Rimrooms contracts, facility procedures, research trees, entities, distortions, room rules, character histories, and campaign outcomes in this catalog. ## Campaign arcs

Each arc adds a new reason to operate the company. Progression is open-ended: contracts, signals, evidence, staffing, and discoveries can provide alternate routes, but new capabilities still need their costs, risks, and recovery rules made visible.

| Arc | Player-facing story | Original-design capabilities and content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Keep the doors open** | Begin with a small facility, five staff, limited stock, and an unfinished gate. Learn to power the site, assign an operator, prepare a crew, and bring home a useful record. | Complete the AI-01 onboarding survey; secure the route recording; choose between Gate Telemetry and another survey. This is the first playable slice. |
| **2. Measure before trusting** | Familiar spaces reveal patterns, but reports disagree. Decide what to measure and which results are reliable enough to use. | Recorders, route tags, beacons, instruments, evidence handling, survey contracts, and the first coordinate atlas. Better measurement improves warnings and planning; it does not reveal a complete explanation. |
| **3. Return with control** | Known destinations become working routes, but every opening costs power, staff time, food, and equipment wear. | Stabilizers, reserve power, return procedures, better communications, route-history tools, repeat expeditions, and limited operating-window extensions. The provisional duration ladder is in the [economy model](CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_MODEL.md). |
| **4. Make a business of it** | Clients request surveys, samples, furniture, instruments, rescue, or secure access. Choose which work is profitable enough to justify its risk. | Procurement, payroll, research deliverables, leases, shipping, salvage, specialist hiring, insurance or compensation candidates, and reputation-based offers. Every quote and contract states its payment, deadline, deliverable, and consequence before acceptance. |
| **5. Build beyond headquarters** | A radio point or shelter can make a return possible where the facility alone cannot reach. Remote sites need people, supplies, signals, protection, and an exit plan. | Relay stations, caches, field shelters, labs, guarded leases, outposts, resupply and evacuation missions, and company-to-site logistics. A remote base is a costly responsibility rather than free map ownership. |
| **6. Respond to the outside world** | Openings appear in towns and ordinary sites. Witnesses, missing residents, public danger, and incomplete reports compete with company interests. | Timed distortion investigations, perimeter control, witness and crew cases, rescue operations, containment decisions, follow-up contracts, and consequences for delay or abandonment. |
| **7. Expand industrial reach** | The company gains ways to move heavy cargo and staff across RimWorld's wider world and, optionally, into space. Gate expeditions remain the defining route into the Backrooms. | Vehicles, ground logistics, gravship-linked cargo and mission records, orbital security or salvage candidates, and off-world support. Odyssey and Vanilla Gravship Expanded content are optional; native gravship ownership stays with its own game/mod systems. |
| **8. Enter deeper systems** | Later coordinates combine known room families and learned hazards, then introduce one unfamiliar rule at a time. No map provides the last word on what the Backrooms are. | Deeper surveys, overlapping route histories, propagated distortions, unusual shelter or industrial networks, higher-risk rescue, and advanced topology/stability research. “Infinite” means renewable seeded destinations, not an unbounded live map. |

The Store and Lone Survivor are separate selectable starts that enter the same coordinate, evidence, threat, and progression systems. The [scenario contract](SCENARIOS.md) defines their current openings and convergence routes. The isolated-outpost, town-distortion, and company-in-crisis starts remain future candidates until given complete scenario cards.

## Research branches, tiers, and unlock themes

Research is **original design** built from evidence, staffed work, projects, contracts, and successful field practice. A bench and a capable researcher still matter, but every result should name a practical capability. Reports and research dossiers can carry knowledge between branches only through the selected, tested exchange route; a discovery does not silently become global multiplayer state.

| Tier | Campaign meaning | Example unlock themes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0 — Facility foundations** | Make the company safe enough to work and establish the first research questions. | Power reserve, machine assembly, repairs, hiring, storage and evidence labels, basic field safety. |
| **1 — First entry** | Prepare a measured short expedition and interpret a first return. | Gate calibration, recorder and survey tags, sample handling, crew readiness, route notes, basic medical response. |
| **2 — Repeatable operations** | Revisit known coordinates and reduce preventable failures. | Gate telemetry, stable openings, better mapping, beacons, radio links, protective gear, analysis and maintenance. |
| **3 — Remote operations** | Support more than one site and accept work beyond headquarters. | Relay stations, supply plans, rescue protocols, leases, outpost defense, longer windows, advanced containment and contract specialization. |
| **4 — Deep operations** | Combine known techniques to enter complex sites and extend company reach. | Topology studies, controlled propagation response, multi-team logistics, high-risk recovery, advanced gate systems, vehicle and optional orbital support. |

These are progression bands, not a promise that every branch has a linear prerequisite chain. Evidence or contract outcomes may open alternate routes. Branch themes are:

| Branch | What it studies | Long-term unlock direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Facilities and power** | Reliable infrastructure, reserve capacity, maintenance, safety, and efficient site operations. | Better generators and reserves, protected rooms, repair practice, and efficient company facilities. |
| **Gate engineering and stability** | Assembly, calibration, gate behavior, opening risk, and recall. | Stabilizers, monitoring, safer recalls, longer windows, and later multi-gate capability. |
| **Fieldcraft and medicine** | Crew readiness, navigation, treatment, exposure, and return preparation. | Improved kits, triage, recovery, training, and more capable rescue teams. |
| **Measurement and evidence** | Recordings, samples, transcripts, instrument readings, provenance, and confidence. | Better sensors, analysis options, evidence comparisons, and reliable client reports. |
| **Spatial mapping and topology** | Coordinate identity, routes, revisits, spatial changes, and generator behavior. | More complete atlases, safer route planning, alternate exits, and deeper-site surveys. |
| **Entities and containment** | Observable threat rules, countermeasures, quarantine, and safe study. | Stronger detection, secure procedures, specialized containment, and carefully scoped study contracts. |
| **Communications and logistics** | Radio quality, signals, shipments, receiving, cargo, and remote support. | Longer-range links, better delivery planning, caches, relays, and outpost resupply. |
| **Commerce and organization** | Contracts, marketable findings, payroll, reputation, procurement, and leases. | Better offers, specialist recruitment, protected commercial sites, and larger operations. |
| **Transport and orbital support** | Moving crews and cargo outside the gate campaign. | Optional vehicle, gravship, and off-world logistics links; native DLC/mod progression remains separate. |

Optional DLC may add familiar routes around these themes, but no branch or required gate capability depends on a DLC. Royalty can offer optional patrons, titles, quests, or psycasts; Ideology can add belief, ritual, or policy interactions; Biotech can add optional medical, gene, mechanoid, or pollution work; Anomaly can add optional containment and research routes; Odyssey can add optional gravship and off-world logistics. Rimrooms keeps a Core-only alternative for every required campaign step, and these layers remain candidates until their exact content and absent-DLC behavior are defined and tested.

## First playable: the opening that teaches the campaign

Keep these values aligned with the [scenario card](SCENARIOS.md), [first playable contract](FIRST_PLAYABLE_CONTRACT.md), [first-slice inventory](FIRST_SLICE_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md), and [threat sheets](THREAT_DESIGN_SHEETS.md). They are provisional balance hypotheses, not tested behavior:

- A 60×60 headquarters with five flexible staff, a provisional $50,000,000 authorized Company Account, 150 physical silver, and the listed starter stock.
- Finish the gate with 100 steel and 8 components; meet the provisional 3,000 W reserve target and keep a qualified operator at the facility.
- Prepare up to three field staff for a 20 in-game-minute opening, with warnings at 10, 5, and 2 minutes.
- Explore AI-01, a saved 6–8-room destination with a validated return path, numbered tags, one Borrowed Corridor route distortion, and one bounded Quiet Pursuer encounter.
- Return and analyze the AI-01 Route Recording. A valid extraction posts one provisional $5,000,000 payment and one research insight. A further $1,000,000 bonus is optional and requires all three field staff and the route, distortion, and entity-observation records to return. A recoverable injury does not cancel it. See [the v0.2 economy model](CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_MODEL.md); amounts are untested balance targets.
- Choose Gate Telemetry or a follow-on survey as the next preparation step.

The distortion cannot injure the crew or move the gate exit. The entity can be avoided or repelled; capture and a kill are not required. The session must remain understandable through text and map cues without relying on color or sound.

## Staff, work, and company spaces

Staff are RimWorld people first. Company roles help direct training and work, but do not replace skills, passions, traits, health, relationships, beliefs, needs, or ordinary autonomy. Recruitment can later include applicants, specialists, contractors, rescued survivors, referrals, and returning staff. Training is earned through supervised work, projects, equipment practice, and field experience.

| Work family | What staff do | Spaces and equipment that support it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Research and analysis** | Analyze route records, compare reports, identify samples, write coordinate profiles, and develop projects. | Research lab, evidence archive, instruments, sample cabinets, report terminals. |
| **Gate operations** | Assemble, calibrate, open, monitor, recall, shut down, and repair the machine. | Guarded gate chamber, console, safety perimeter, cutoff, stabilizers, coordinate recorder. |
| **Engineering and fabrication** | Maintain power, repair equipment, build modules, and prepare field tools. | Power room, workshop, generator, battery, switchgear, repair and fabrication benches. |
| **Security and field operations** | Guard access, escort crews, mark routes, survey rooms, place beacons, and recover cargo. | Armory, training area, field kits, protective equipment, tags, radios, tether, beacon. |
| **Medical and containment** | Treat injuries, decontaminate, examine evidence, isolate uncertain arrivals, and monitor secure rooms. | Medical bay, quarantine/decontamination, secure cells, restraints, medical kits, sealed containers. |
| **Logistics and communications** | Receive shipments, pack expeditions, maintain radios, report signals, and supply remote sites. | Receiving bay, storage, dispatch/radio room, cargo frames, relays, inventory board. |
| **Administration and contracts** | Hire, schedule, quote orders, accept work, document results, and reconcile payments. | Operations board, office, project board, contract files, branch ledger. |
| **Care and recovery** | Keep shifts, meals, sleep, recreation, treatment, and return-to-work legible. | Dormitory, cafeteria/mess, recreation, infirmary, changing and decontamination areas. |

Facility functions should be visible through buildings, stock, assigned staff, and access controls—not hidden room scores. Rooms include gate control, power and maintenance, lab, archive, decontamination, medical, armory, workshop, radio/dispatch, receiving/storage, staff quarters, cafeteria, and recreation. Later branches may add secure leases, outpost infrastructure, and vehicle or gravship staging.

## Equipment and build families

These are original-design families; individual pieces, art, costs, and unlocks remain content records to be authored and balanced.

- **Gate and power:** frame, control console, calibrated array, emergency cutoff, stabilizer modules, power reserve, monitoring, maintenance tools, and later multi-gate infrastructure.
- **Navigation and return:** numbered survey tags, route recorder, map board, tether, return beacon, signal triangulator, relay radio, and upgraded route-history instruments.
- **Protection and response:** uniforms, protective clothing, armor, appropriate weapons, lights, medical packs, restraints, portable shielding or barriers as candidates, and sealed transport cases.
- **Research and evidence:** cameras/recorders, sample jars, scanners, measuring instruments, evidence containers, archive cabinets, lab fixtures, transcript and dossier records.
- **Cargo and logistics:** field packs, cargo frames, salvage restraints, receiving pallets, cold or secure storage, shipment kits, supply drops, and outpost caches.
- **Life and work:** beds, food preparation, recreation, training props, shift tools, uniforms, safety signs, furniture, and replacement parts.
- **Transport and space:** company vehicles and service equipment as candidates; optional gravship links use their native ship, crew, and operating systems rather than replacing them.

Every item description should tell the player its practical effect—such as protection, information, range, capacity, signal quality, or return reliability. If no effect is defined, it is set dressing, not a promised progression item.

## Evidence, people, and casework

Evidence turns an expedition into knowledge, a contract result, or a difficult company decision. Candidate evidence includes route maps and tags, radio fragments, recorder files and transcripts, instrument readings, room photographs as original in-game records, environmental samples, entity traces or remains, recovered furniture/equipment, and witness or crew accounts.

The player can secure, label, quarantine, analyze, archive, compare, deliver to a client, trade or sell eligible material, use it for research, or dispose of it. Each case shows where the evidence came from, who handled it, its condition and risk, what is known, what remains uncertain, and which choices will consume or transfer it. Physical objects remain physical cargo; case and analysis histories remain in the local branch. A traded dossier is a recipient's copy, not authority over the sender's case or research.

People encountered in the spaces are tracked as people and cases, not anonymous cargo stacks. Depending on the incident and explicit player choice, they may be rescued, treated, interviewed, hired, hosted as guests, detained, quarantined, released, returned, or made the subject of a defined paid investigation. Any transfer, custody, or research outcome must state its purpose and consequence; do not imply an automatic result from merely finding someone.

## Contracts, incidents, and mysteries

All generated mission variations are bounded original design: authored objective patterns combine with saved coordinate history, client needs, company capability, crew readiness, available gear, and declared risks. A brief names what counts as success, what may be lost, any deadline, payment, bonus, penalty, and cancellation rule. A partial result pays only when the offer says so.

| Family | Typical assignment and story pressure |
| --- | --- |
| **Survey and mapping** | Record rooms, exits, route changes, light, hazards, and signal quality; an interrupted report leaves an unresolved coordinate clue. |
| **Retrieval and salvage** | Recover requested equipment, furniture, samples, or records; carrying limits and contamination compete with value. |
| **Research delivery** | Return a named recording, transcript, instrument reading, or sample; clients may disagree about what the evidence proves. |
| **Rescue and missing crew** | Follow last-known tags, radio fragments, contradictory time reports, and partial sightings. A crew may be found alive, injured, separated, late, dead, or still missing; reports retain a next lead when one exists. |
| **Reappearance and death case** | A person or crew returns after an unexplained interval, with changed equipment, a conflicting account, an injury, or a confirmed death report. The company can compare records, interview witnesses, examine evidence, offer aid, or close the case unresolved. No unexplained outcome is silently converted into a random reward. |
| **Containment and security** | Secure a person, object, route, or threat under stated conditions; alarms, capacity, staffing, escape risk, and release decisions create follow-up work. |
| **Town or settlement distortion** | A timed opening creates a perimeter and public-safety problem: locate missing residents, preserve witness accounts, decide who may enter, recover evidence, and stabilize, monitor, or abandon the site. Faction pressure and local trust can change with the outcome. |
| **Commercial and site work** | Accept a lease, build or defend a relay, fulfill an order, deliver supplies, secure salvage rights, or document a client's use of a space. Rent and operating costs begin only when the offer or lease says they do. |
| **Outpost and transport support** | Establish, supply, repair, evacuate, or reconnect a post; later, move approved cargo or support an optional gravship operation. |

Mystery is delivered through records that disagree, gaps in timing, changed landmarks, witnesses with limited knowledge, and consequences that make another investigation worthwhile. The company can form hypotheses; an incomplete dossier must remain visibly incomplete. Death follows ordinary RimWorld risks and is recorded as loss with a case trail, not used as a forced opening beat.

## Threat and anomaly families

The first-slice threats are fixed in [THREAT_DESIGN_SHEETS.md](THREAT_DESIGN_SHEETS.md): the **Borrowed Corridor** is a one-time, recoverable route mismatch, and the **Quiet Pursuer** is an original, bounded encounter whose observation, retreat, and repel choices do not require a kill or capture.

Later content may draw from these original-design families. They are candidates, not a promised complete roster:

- **Pursuit and territorial entities:** announce their presence through readable tells, follow limited rules, and have at least one learnable countermeasure.
- **Environmental anomalies:** altered connections, repeated rooms, shifting landmarks, misleading exits, signal dead zones, time-report discrepancies, or objects that move according to a discoverable rule.
- **Propagation events:** a spatial or environmental change may affect nearby rooms in visible, logged stages; its reach, trigger, warning, and stopping condition are stated.
- **Human and company threats:** panic, misinformation, unsafe experiments, breached custody, sabotage, or a client who wants more than the crew can safely deliver.
- **World-site incidents:** unstable openings, witness exposure, failed evacuations, communications loss, and threats attempting to cross a monitored threshold.
- **Deep-site and optional space threats:** hazards around isolated sites or transport missions; they do not become Backrooms entities solely because gravships are present.

Every shipped threat needs its own tells, trigger, limits, counterplay, evidence, outcomes, accessibility cues, and recovery contract before implementation. Capture, study, transfer, or sale is available only where that threat's contract defines the equipment, staff, risks, and custody result. Follow the [procedural space contract](PROCEDURAL_SPACE_CONTRACT.md): never let an unannounced event close every return route or silently reroll a saved destination.

## Room archetypes and spatial variation

Rooms combine familiar functions with unsettling but legible differences. The [procedural space contract](PROCEDURAL_SPACE_CONTRACT.md) owns coordinate identity, saved generation inputs, return rules, and migration; the [visual/audio style brief](research/VISUAL_AUDIO_STYLE_BRIEF.md) owns presentation guidance.

| Archetype family | Example use |
| --- | --- |
| **Threshold and survey** | Safe entry/return point, survey lobby, junction, marker and first map. |
| **Office and records** | Offices, copy rooms, filing spaces, interview rooms, document caches, and recorder leads. |
| **Retail and domestic** | Showroom, storeroom, apartment-like rooms, kitchens, bedrooms, waiting areas, and familiar furniture. |
| **Service and transit** | Corridors, stairwells, elevators as candidates, loading areas, utility passages, and dead ends that are not required exits. |
| **Industrial and power** | Mechanical rooms, workshops, plant rooms, cable routes, substations, and maintenance spaces. |
| **Medical and institutional** | Clinic-like rooms, laboratories, examination spaces, quarantine areas, and abandoned work areas. |
| **Shelter and communications** | Rest points, security posts, relay stations, supply rooms, forward shelters, and outpost hubs. |
| **Unfinished and altered** | Construction shells, repeated rooms, impossible adjacency, shifted doorways, altered dimensions, and changed room identity. |
| **Deep or orbital support** | Later transit and logistics spaces connected to company expansion; orbital content remains a separate optional layer. |

Variation is seeded and saved. Each destination has a known entry, observed map, landmarks, changes, and return knowledge. Early runs use a small room count and one unfamiliar major rule at a time. Later sites can recombine known rules before adding another. Generation must keep required objectives reachable and a recoverable exit; “procedural” does not mean arbitrary or disposable.

## Outposts, vehicles, and space expansion

Outposts are a second network to maintain, not an independent colony game disconnected from the gate. Candidate types include radio relays, supply caches, crew shelters, guarded survey stations, temporary laboratories, quarantine posts, construction or lease sites, and recovery points. Each site has staffing, stock, communications, upkeep, defense, route quality, and evacuation information. A failed link creates a mission or loss report; it does not erase the outpost's history.

Ground vehicles and later off-world transport may extend cargo, rescue range, and company logistics. **Optional** Odyssey and Vanilla Gravship Expanded Chapters 1 and 2 can provide a later native gravship layer when present and verified. Rimrooms may show a branch-local mission link and reconcile an explicitly supported cargo receipt; native gravship ownership, ship operations, crew, and state remain with their owning content. Core-only gate exploration remains complete without vehicles, Odyssey, or either gravship chapter.

## Language, interface, and accessibility

English is the first player-facing language; every label, alert, briefing, clue, and outcome is localization-ready. The Operations board connects Overview, Personnel, Facilities, Gate, Expeditions, Atlas, Evidence/Research, and Contracts/Ledger while preserving familiar RimWorld Work, Architect, Assign, Research, World, and pawn controls.

Show names beside unfamiliar icons. Pair danger, ownership, route changes, gate countdowns, and availability with text and shape/icon/map cues, never color alone. Pair radio or threat audio with persistent text or map information. Critical warnings remain readable after their first notice; the player can pause and review them. Reduced flashes/shake and muted or lowered repeated ambience should preserve every gameplay clue. Test normal zoom, UI scaling, long localized strings, keyboard focus, and no-sound play before release.
