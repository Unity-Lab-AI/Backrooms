**Owner direction, 2026-10-08, verbatim:** *"think massive perfectly set up working facility and machine gate operations of using the bacvkrooms for cash like stripping and taking things to sell and completing misisons ect ect BUILD YOUR EMPIRE UNITY MAKE A RIMWORLD PLAY SCRIPT?.MD"*

**And, verbatim:** *"and your gate need to be not just a side room it needs to be in a massive facility with all it supposrt structures in a windowed off room for security ect ect and security   u can do the research as u need it"*

This is the **script**: the order a run is played in, act by act, each act with what it must reach before the next starts. [`PLAYBOOK.md`](PLAYBOOK.md) holds every owner order verbatim plus the how-tos and the don'ts it was learned from; this file is the running order built on it. [`NOW.md`](NOW.md) says which act the live colony is on and which TEST row it closes. **Keep all three current as the run is played** -- a new how-to, a thing that works or a thing that failed goes into PLAYBOOK the moment it is learned, and if it changes the order it changes this script.

## Every in-game day, whatever act it is

Read letters the moment they arrive; trade with every trader (comms console or the pawn); food on shelves, freezer cold; power on every building by conduit; generators fuelled and outside; crops harvested and resown; work tab per PLAYBOOK §4c (1s for firefight to cook, 2s for each pawn's own job, one cell at a time, read back); leader and moral guide abilities used; nobody melees, draft and group up on any attack. PLAYBOOK §0-§4.

## Act 1 -- Land and survive (days 1-5)

**Reach:** nobody hungry, cold or hurt; roof over beds; food for 10 days.

1. World gen: 300x300, Spring, a tile with trees and stone (PLAYBOOK §8).
2. Growing zone (rice/potato) + cook bill + butcher; wood and stone in; shelves for every resource one priority above the floor zone.
3. Freezer with coolers blue-in, red-out; generator outdoors, battery joined, conduit to everything.
4. Work tab, schedules, leader and moral guide set.

## Act 2 -- Sustainable (days 5-20)

**Reach:** positive power, steady meals, medicine, recreation, a cash crop in the ground. **Nothing gate-related before this** (PLAYBOOK §00).

1. Cash crops: smokeleaf -> joints, psychoid -> flake/yayo, devilstrand; cotton for clothes.
2. Animals: a fenced pen, milk animals tamed or bought.
3. Hospital (sterile tile), bedrooms (wood floor, vents, lights, double beds), rec room.
4. Research in order, **building each upgrade as it lands, removing the outdated bench**: Microelectronics -> trade beacon + multi-analyzer -> hi-tech bench -> computing line. Then **Drug production -> drug lab**, and the rest **as each is needed** (owner: *"u can do the research as u need it"*).

## Act 3 -- The fortress (alongside Act 2)

**Reach:** one perimeter, one killbox entrance, towers on every corner.

1. Architect plan written first (PLAYBOOK §01): 3-wide halls, rooms by purpose, courtyards unroofed, no walls on water or against unmined rock.
2. **Build in steel** where it matters; **order steel from the company** (Operations -> Procurement) when the pile runs low.
3. Killbox entrance: staggered sandbags, turrets, embrasures.
4. **A tower on every corner** with embrasures to shoot out from, **its own ventilation**, **security rooms** (armory, guard post, med kit) and **embrasure sally ports** (PLAYBOOK §01 goal 9).
5. **Prison**: cells with beds, a commons, locked doors; capture raiders, recruit or put them to work behind layers of locks and walls (PLAYBOOK §6).

## Act 4 -- The money machine (alongside Act 3)

**Reach:** silver coming in every season without a gate.

1. **Trade and sell goods and product** -- every caravan, every orbital trader via beacon.
2. **Drug lab**: make what can be made, **acquire the ingredients** (grow or buy), sell the product.
3. **Guests**: sell zone through the Guests tab with the processed goods only (joints, not leaves); beauty, good food and guest beds so they stay and buy; recruit from them.
4. **Company missions for cash** (Operations -> Requests and contracts).

## Act 5 -- The gate complex (massive, not a side room)

**Reach:** a gate inside a secured facility with every support structure, and a connection open.

Layout, deepest inside the perimeter, behind its own locked entry:

| Room | What is in it |
|------|----------------|
| **Gate chamber** | the gate door, nothing else; steel walls; turrets covering the door from outside the chamber |
| **Control room, windowed off** | communications console and operator chair, looking into the chamber through **windows** (*Pass It Through The Window*) and **embrasures** -- the operator sees the gate and can shoot at whatever comes through, behind a wall |
| **Power room** | battery bank (vanilla `Battery` for the gate bind) and its own conduit loop, a switch for the emergency cutoff |
| **Assembly shop** | up to four machining tables linked to the gate, steel and components on shelves beside them |
| **Receiving bay** | shelves linked as the gate's receiving bay -- everything that comes back lands here |
| **Records archive** | shelves for books, linked as *Records archive* at Critical |
| **Laboratory** | hi-tech research bench as the company laboratory for analysis |
| **Quarantine / med bay** | beds and medicine for anyone who comes back wrong |
| **Security room** | armory racks, guard post, the gate's own sally port to the chamber |
| **Crew quarters** | operator and expedition crew beds, so the operator never walks across the base to sleep (PLAYBOOK §9 step 8b) |

Commission and bring it up per PLAYBOOK §10: commission the door -> table to gate control -> **Assemble gate** x4 -> assign operator -> calibrate -> console to gate control -> remember an address -> staff the console -> open a connection. The **Emergency abort -- do not open** button on the load notice is the way out of a bad address.

## Act 6 -- Gate operations for cash

**Reach:** every trip pays.

1. **Surveys** (PLAYBOOK §9): send a crew, record observations, come home, file the record, analyse to 100 % -> payout.
2. **Stripping**: deconstruct and haul what is down there -- furniture, lights, steel, components -- back through the gate into the receiving bay, and **sell** it.
3. **Missions and requests**: each repeat needs **a different address** (owner's rule). Take every one the crew can survive.
4. Treat everything down there per *"remember lsd unnerving feeling with all things"* -- staff on the console the whole trip, recall early, never send someone alone who cannot walk back.

## Act 7 -- Empire

**Reach:** more of everything, and every faction an ally.

Stockpiles of everything, a vault, armies and turrets; children once the colony has adults (private double-bed rooms, then cribs, milk, toys); ideology statues, party room, throne room, landing pads; **gifts by drop pod** to every faction until they are allies; more sites, more gates, more crews.
