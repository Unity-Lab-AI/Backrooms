## Player route and state

Operations → Facilities reads the existing HQ's spawned, visible, player-owned buildings. A data-driven classification groups quarters, medical furniture, cafeteria/kitchen, recreation, labs, workshops, machine, power and storage; unknown furniture remains inspectable under Unclassified. This creates no physical content or assets. The legacy RR gate/lab names remain historical bindings until the replacement map is implemented.

The report shows actual bed slots, owners and occupants as separate counts, with medical and other/specialized beds separated. Staff with no owned bed and downed/healing staff are listed. It does not claim an empty slot is suitable or reachable, assign/evict bed owners, alter medical treatment, change needs, or infer a room capability from its label. Native Assign and the real selected building remain reachable.

Building rows show condition, native room label, cell/roof/temperature observations, power where a native trader exists, and the interaction cell's immediate standability. The latter is explicitly not pawn-specific reachability/reservation clearance. A machine row links back to its authoritative Operations pane.

## Actual source and output

- [FacilityReport.cs](../../src/RimroomsAsyncIndustries/Facilities/FacilityReport.cs): transient scalar observations plus physical building references; no saved rooms or duplicate facility simulation.
- [OperationsFacilities.cs](../../src/RimroomsAsyncIndustries/UI/OperationsFacilities.cs): category selection, 12-row pagination, explicit refresh and revalidated native inspection.
- [Classification Defs](../../Mod/Rimrooms%20-%20Async%20Industries/1.6/Defs/RimroomsFacilityDefs/RR_FacilityCategories.xml): configuration of existing provider names, not new buildings or clones.
- [Keyed text](../../Mod/Rimrooms%20-%20Async%20Industries/1.6/Languages/English/Keyed/RR_Facilities.xml): explanations, actions and limited-observation warnings.

The transient cache refreshes only while the panel is used, after HQ changes, after a tick rewind, every 250 game ticks or on explicit refresh. No map is generated by this view. Destroyed/fogged/moved buildings are rechecked before selection. A failing optional furniture getter increments a visible incomplete-observation count instead of discarding the entire report. The diagnostic timer is `facilities-summary`; no measurement has been collected.
