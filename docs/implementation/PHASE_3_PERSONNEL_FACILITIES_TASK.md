## Proposed next increment

Prepare two small implementation steps: a read-only HQ capability/needs report, followed by a bounded applicant board for voluntary company hires. Keep ordinary Work, Assign, pawn health/needs/skills and building controls available. ### A. Physical HQ and staff report

Operations → Facilities opens the branch HQ and groups its **existing, spawned, player-owned, unfogged** buildings by function. Inspect selects the real building/pawn; an unloaded, removed or unavailable HQ reports that state without generating a map. Room labels are descriptive. A room can support several functions. Do not require a room name, impressiveness or hidden score to unlock company work.

| Initial report | Show from physical state | Boundary / useful next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quarters | Ordinary adult humanlike colonist-bed slots, assigned owners, actual occupants, medical/prisoner/slave/baby categories separately; roof/outdoor/temperature facts | A raw empty slot is not a promise that every pawn can use it. Select a bed or open native assignment. Show staff without an owned bed separately. Do not evict owners or assign beds automatically. |
| Medical | Actual medical beds and occupants, injured/downed staff, native care/health access | This is treatment infrastructure, not proof of adequate doctors, medicine, isolation or safety. Native medical actions remain authoritative. |
| Mess and recreation | Tables/seating, native cooking/worktable facilities, recognized recreation furniture, staff food/rest/joy/mood readings | Presence is not a guarantee of nutrition, access or an executable bill. Select the actual building or pawn. No automatic meals or per-meal USD debit. |
| Lab/workshop | Native research/work benches, `RR_FieldAnalysisBench`, existing power/interaction-cell status where relevant | Distinguish present, powered and blocked; do not claim research capability merely from a room role or reset native research. Use the existing analysis service for its real readiness result. |
| Machine/power | Existing gate, console, cutoff and power buildings; use the current gate component's status/refusal text | Read the authoritative gate/power APIs already reviewed in Phase 2. Do not compute a second reserve or opening timer. |

Start with a data-driven Core/Rimrooms building catalogue. Unrecognized optional buildings remain selectable as **unclassified**, with their Def label; they are not silently equivalent to a lab, safe cell or guest-free bed. Modded `Building_Bed` subclasses can contribute a separately labeled physical-slot count, while per-pawn usability remains a native query. No optional-package strings should be hard dependencies. Quarantine/security certification, archive custody, receiving/storage throughput, radio networks and outposts retain their own later implementation tasks; a furniture report does not implement those services.

Personnel continues to display the actual staff pawn, saved role and wage. Add food/rest/joy/mood where present, health/downed/dead/missing condition, owned bed, current native schedule and skill/disabled-work information. Missing needs or trackers display **not applicable/unavailable**, never zero health or an invented need. Present ratios as observations and do not alter needs, thoughts, ideology, timetable or job queues while drawing.

Cache summaries only while the panel is in use, invalidating on HQ change and refreshing on a bounded game-tick interval (proposed 250 ticks) or explicit refresh. Do not scan every map every tick. Cache building IDs and scalar observations, not durable `Room` references. Selected-pawn reachability/reservation queries belong to an explicit inspection, not an all-pawns × all-beds tick loop. A stale summary must recheck the target before a click.

### B. Ordinary voluntary applicants

Operations → Personnel → Applicants shows a bounded list of generated, inspectable people: name, age, actual skills/passions, traits, health, work restrictions, suggested company role, actual included apparel/items, onboarding fee, daily wage and first billing boundary. **Hire** explicitly confirms the saved quote; insufficient funds, missing HQ, unsafe/unreachable map-edge arrival, invalid candidate ownership or an unfinished previous hiring journal gives a concrete refusal and leaves the offer unchanged.

Use an explicit **Request applicants** action after a branch exists. Proposed editable bounds are three active offers, no more than one generation call per simulation tick, a seven-game-day offer/refresh interval, and no refresh bypass by repeatedly opening the panel. These are routine implementation/balance proposals, not owner-fixed values. The native generator has its own bounded retries; do not wrap it in an unbounded search for perfect traits or skills. Advance a saved generation attempt receipt before calling it; a failed slot remains visibly failed until the next permitted request, rather than rerolling every tick.

Generate ordinary adult human candidates once with a Core kind, a constructed `PawnGenerationRequest`, `forceGenerateNewPawn: true`, `faction: null`, `context: NonPlayer`, `canGeneratePawnRelations: false`, `allowDead: false`, `allowDowned: false`, and no forced ideology/traits/skills. Preserve native variation and show role fit; a weak or disabled skill is not repaired by the Hire button. Restrict this first board to its own newly generated candidates. Revalidate the returned pawn and required trackers because Core may relax generation validators and optional mods can alter generation. New specialist kinds and advertised guaranteed skill floors need their own later records and strict post-validation.

Use the existing economy hypotheses as the ordinary offer's explicit defaults: **$100,000 onboarding and $5,000/day**, quoted separately and stored before acceptance. Specialist premiums, contractors and other terms are outside this step. The fee is one ledger debit, not silver or a fee per visible skill. Display that the first full daily wage falls at the next HQ payroll boundary, matching current payroll behavior; no hidden retrospective days or second payroll timer. A new hire is added to payroll only after physical arrival and successful branch registration. The existing five staff, balance, obligations and earlier billing receipts must remain unchanged.

The initial arrival route is an available ordinary **HQ edge walk-in**, not a gate transfer, drop pod or new shipment system. Find a native acceptable edge cell before charging; additionally reject occupied/unsafe cells or cells that would wipe an edifice. Spawn the **same saved applicant pawn** there, complete native voluntary recruitment once, register it once and let ordinary pawn work/pathing operate. The receipt says arrival occurred at the edge; it does not promise that a particular room or bed has been reached. Lack of quarters is a visible welfare warning, not an invented hard hiring gate: the player can use ordinary RimWorld sleeping/medical decisions.

Role selection changes the branch role record only. Show recommended native work types and link Work/Assign; **do not automatically apply priority presets in this first increment**. If a later explicit Apply recommendation action is implemented, preview changes, skip disabled/missing types, preserve every unlisted priority and leave manual-priority mode unchanged. Training/certification and experience awards remain later tasks.

## Save, quote and recovery design

Extend the existing branch owner, rather than registering every native colony pawn as an employee. The current schema is 2; the proposed records below require an explicit schema-3 migration and source/save-policy update during implementation. Migrate 1 → 2 → 3 in order, preserving every existing ID, balance, staff reference, role, wage, obligation, expedition and site. Initialize new board collections/counters empty. Constructors, loading and migration generate no candidates and charge nothing. Ordinary non-Rimrooms saves remain inactive. Unknown schema stays mutation-disabled.

| Planned record | Stable fields / authority |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant board | Branch-owned monotonic sequence, request batch ID/attempt index, policy Def/version, next request tick, bounded offered candidates, pending generation/release/hire receipts; `ThingOwner<Pawn>` for actual off-site candidates |
| Applicant offer | `<branch>:applicant:<sequence>`, actual pawn reference plus `GetUniqueLoadID()`, name snapshot, source kind, suggested role, quote version, onboarding USD, daily wage USD, issued/expires game ticks, state/reason; reference only to a deep-owned or native-owned pawn |
| Hiring journal | `<applicant>:hire:1`, target HQ/map identity and preflight cell, accepted terms, ledger operation `<hire>:onboarding`, stages Accepted/Charged/Arrived/Recruited/Registered or RecoveryRequired; observed physical owner/reference and failure details |
| Registered employee | Stable `StaffRecord.id` derived from hire ID, native pawn reference/ID, name at hire, selected role, quoted daily wage, actual hired tick, employed flag. Never use list count, pawn display name or current kind as identity. |
| Facility snapshot | Transient HQ/map plus building IDs, observations and snapshot tick. `Room.ID` and inferred capacity are not durable state. Missing links preserve the last identified target with an unavailable message. |

**Ordered hire service:** validate supported active branch, offered/unexpired saved quote, unique own applicant, funds, live HQ and clear native arrival route. Create/update the stable journal first. Debit the onboarding receipt once using current `PostTransaction`. Spawn the same held pawn at the saved/revalidated HQ edge. Recruit through the native utility only if not yet player-owned. Register one staff record only when that actual pawn is spawned at HQ and belongs to the player. Subsequent retries inspect the recorded pawn and completed stages; they do not issue another fee, pawn, recruit notification or staff record. Do not modify a person discovered to be held/controlled by another system.

Every catch must reconcile reality: a thrown SpawnSetup can leave the pawn spawned, held, or detached. Retain a strong reference throughout; if still unspawned and ownerless, return that **same** pawn to the recovery holder. If spawned, preserve it where it is and do not despawn it merely to roll back payment. A charged interrupted hire has explicit **Retry arrival/registration** with its original quote and no second debit. Offer **Cancel and refund** only while the original pawn is safely off-site, not player-recruited, not registered and no physical arrival occurred; refund has its own one-time receipt and the pawn is released to the native world owner once. A refunded/cancelled ID can never be reactivated. Missing pawn/ambiguous physical ownership blocks retry/refund until the actual state is reconciled; never create a replacement to repair the record.

Decline/expiry similarly uses a saved release journal: verify only our own held unspawned pawn, remove it from the holder and pass to native world ownership once; if the transition throws, retain/reconcile the same reference. Terminal history keeps identifying snapshots and tolerates later native world-pawn GC; terminal reference loss is not a reason to regenerate. All active offers/pending hires stay physically owned exactly once. Do not keep every historical applicant deep-held forever.

Current payroll totals employed, living, non-destroyed staff at the existing daily cutoff and posts idempotent obligations. Missing/dead/departed staff remain visible history. Do not silently fire missing expedition crew, double-charge the onboarding fee as salary, or start a separate wage counter. Employment termination/release and specialist contract policy remain later scope; show current native faction/location changes as unavailable employment actions until their explicit disposition is implemented.
