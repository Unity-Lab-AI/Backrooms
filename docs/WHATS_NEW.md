# What's new

Written for players. If you want the short version: **this is a development build and no part of
it has been played yet.** Everything below is built and checked against the game's own code. None
of it has been judged in a real colony.

## Where the mod stands

**It needs nothing.** Not Harmony, not an expansion, not another mod. RimWorld Core on its own is
the supported way to play it, and the long list of mods it knows how to sit beside is sorting
advice rather than a requirement.

**It is not a finished campaign.** The company, the gates, the coordinates, the research, the
requests and the ledger are all there and all connected. Whether they are *fun* is the one
question nobody has answered, because nobody has played a full run.

## Recently

**The levels got bigger, and deeper levels got bigger than shallow ones.** A coordinate can now
hold up to eighty rooms. Depth one and two deliberately did not change — near the surface the
place is meant to be fewer, larger rooms, and that look is protected. From the third level down
you will find more of them, further apart.

**Several grand rooms per level instead of one.** Three near the surface, two below that, one deep
down. Each is linked into the level as it is built, so a grand room always has at least two ways
out of it.

**The free ways through reach the sixth level.** Not the third. If you have read anywhere that
they stop at three, that was wrong and it has been corrected.

**A notice before the screen freezes, and one when it is over.** Building a level takes a moment
and the game stops while it happens. You now get told before it starts and told again when the
place is ready, so a pause never looks like a crash.

**You can change what the company pays you.** Four sliders in the mod's settings: what it pays for
things that came out of a coordinate, what it pays for ordinary valuables, the better rate a
trained branch earns, and the fee on the supply catalogue. **The shipped numbers are the
defaults**, so if you never open the settings nothing is different.

**Settings no longer show controls for work you do not have.** If you are playing without Anomaly,
you will not see a priority slider for crossing a gate to study a contained entity.

**A gate can be set to gate control, and that is usually the answer.** Two of the eleven startup
steps — the table and the console — have to be set to gate control separately. If you have done
everything and a gate still will not open, that is the first thing to check. It is now written
down in the guide, where it should always have been.

## What to read

The guide covers the whole mod, page by page: what a gate is, how a connection works, what you
find down there, what the company wants, and what to do when something refuses. Start at the
front page.

## Known, and said out loud

- **Nothing is claimed as tested with other mods.** It loads beside a lot of them. That is not the
  same as working with them, and it will not be claimed until somebody has run it.
- **Co-op is not promised.** Playing alongside another colony needs RimWorld Together, which has
  its own requirements. Whether this mod behaves correctly across it has not been established.
- **Saves may break between development builds.** While the version number starts with `0.`, a new
  build is not promised to open an old save. The build that wrote a save can always open it, so
  keep it if you want to go back.
- **Balance is unjudged.** Costs, payments, timings and difficulty are first passes. The sliders
  exist so you do not have to wait for somebody else to decide.
