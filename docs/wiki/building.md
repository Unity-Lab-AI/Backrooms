# What you can build

Rimrooms adds its own buildings, one floor and one item. **None of them is required.** Every job
below can also be done with something the base game already ships, and a colony that never builds
any of it works exactly the same.

They are here because the company has its own kit, not because the mod stopped working without it.

## Power and light

| Build | Where | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **company utility generator** | Power | Wood-fired. More output than a standard set for the same footprint, and it eats and breaks for it | Electricity |
| **emergency cutoff** | Power | A sealed stop switch on a conduit run. Opens the circuit exactly as an ordinary power switch does | Electricity |
| **liminal fluorescent fixture** | Furniture | A long strip light. Throws a wide flat pool down a corridor instead of a bright circle | Electricity |
| **site marker beacon** | Furniture | A small amber light, meant to mark a spot rather than light a room | Electricity |

The generator is the trade worth reading twice: **more watts, more wood, more breakdowns.** A branch
running one is usually a branch that could not reach a grid.

## Gate equipment

| Build | Where | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **company gate console** | Misc | A working communications console that can also be bound to a gate as its control station | Microelectronics basics |
| **field analysis bench** | Production | A work table that can be bound to a gate as its assembly bench | Machining |

**Neither replaces the ordinary version.** An ordinary communications console and an ordinary
machining table still do these jobs, and always will.

**Building one settles a choice rather than creating one.** If you have exactly one of the company's
own, the gate takes it without asking, however many ordinary ones are standing nearby.

## Field kit

| Build | Where | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **field recorder and radio** | Production | Stand it beside a field analysis bench and that bench works faster. One per bench | Microelectronics basics |
| **survey tag** | Misc | A marker you set and give a meaning, read the same way as a glow pod | — |
| **sealed evidence case** | Furniture | Storage that accepts recordings and journals and nothing else | — |

**The survey tag differs from a glow pod in the one way that matters underground.** A glow pod is
alive and dies on its own schedule. A tag does not.

**The evidence case is worth it for what it refuses.** A shelf will take whatever a hauler decides
belongs there; a case will not, so what is inside it is always what it says it is.

All three are one cell and can be uninstalled and carried, so a crew can take one through a gate and
set it down on the other side.

## Floors

| Build | Where | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **faded institutional carpet** | Floors | The straw-coloured wall-to-wall carpet the places out there are floored with | Carpet making |

Same cloth cost, same research and same cleaning penalty as any other carpet. What is ours is the
surface.

## Made at a bench

| Make | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **company route recording** | field analysis bench | A blank company journal. Take it out empty, write it in the field, bring it home as the record |

The company also posts these out to a branch that has a gate and none. Making your own is the faster
route if you have a bench.

## A note on which way things face

Some of these do not rotate. That is deliberate and not an oversight: a building only rotates when
every side of it has actually been drawn, and for several of these only the front exists.

Shipping a rotatable building with one picture would show a missing-texture square on three sides
out of four, and you would only find out after placing it.
