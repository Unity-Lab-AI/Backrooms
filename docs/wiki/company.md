# The company

The company is not a faction you befriend. It is an account, a queue of requests, and a bill.

---

## Nothing the company asks for has a deadline

**There are no deadlines in this mod.** No request, contract, offer, quote or trade ever expires.
There is no penalty for taking a long time, no cancellation for slowness, and nothing that asks you
to respond within a set period.

You may cancel a job. The corporation may not.

The only thing in this mod with a duration is **a gate's open connection**, and that duration is a
consequence of power, technology, maintenance and staffing — all things you control. See
[Gates and connections](gates.md).

## Every job has more than one way to finish

Every request and contract offers **at least two routes to success, of at least two different
kinds**. The kinds are:

| Route | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Deliver** | Bring the thing back |
| **Document** | Bring the *record* back instead of the thing |
| **Substitute** | Satisfy it with a different item of the same capability |
| **Purchase** | Buy the shortfall through procurement |
| **Testify** | A staff member's account, where a crew saw what a record would show |
| **Decline and redirect** | Refuse the stated task and complete a related one |
| **Research** | Complete a named company project |

The corporation does not care how a branch succeeds.

---

## Two kinds of money

| | |
|---|---|
| **The account** | A ledger in dollars. Pays staff, site fees, procurement, obligations. Never spawned as silver. |
| **Physical stock** | Silver, steel, food, gear, salvage. Ordinary RimWorld goods, behaving exactly as always. |

The Ledger pane lists recent movements with a reason for each. Unpaid obligations do not quietly
vanish — pay them from Overview.

## Bonds

The account can be printed as **bearer bonds**, in denominations from ten up to a quadrillion.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Named for their value** | A bond says what it is worth |
| **Deposit** | Put one back in, from the paper itself, at full value |
| **Combine** | Fold several into the fewest |
| **A beacon** | Banks every bond in a radius at once, also at full value |

A bond is not sold. It is already credits, so banking it is what happens to it — the company
declines to buy one, because selling a bond at a discount would lose you money by accident.

**Ordinary valuables are a separate trade.** Sell those to the company and you receive **85%** of
their worth: instant, with no trader, caravan or travel, and the company takes a cut for that.

Paper raises colony wealth where the ledger does not. Liquidity costs risk, and paper burns.

## Requests and contracts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Request** | What the corporation asks for next. Finish one and the next opens. |
| **Contract** | A priced job with terms, a base payment and a bonus. |

**The opening is a tutorial that becomes a campaign.** Six requests teach the systems in dependency
order — power the gate, assemble and calibrate it, bring back one record, mark a route home, report
a disagreement, hold a connection open.

The seventh is **the hinge**: the corporation asks you to choose a direction. You may declare one,
or go and do the work in one without announcing anything. Every choice succeeds.

**After the hinge the corporation keeps asking, and stops teaching.** Later requests are generated
from your branch's state, your coordinate history and what you can do.

The Contracts pane shows both, requests first.

## The arcs

The campaign is laid out in eight arcs. Each adds a kind of work rather than a difficulty step.

| | |
|---|---|
| **1. Keep the doors open** | Power the site, assign an operator, bring home a record |
| **2. Measure before trusting** | Familiar spaces reveal patterns; reports disagree |
| **3. Return with control** | Known destinations become working routes |
| **4. Make a business of it** | Clients request surveys, samples, instruments, rescue, access |
| **5. Build beyond headquarters** | Remote sites need people, supplies, signals, protection, an exit |
| **6. Respond to the outside world** | Openings appear in towns; witnesses and missing residents |
| **7. Expand industrial reach** | Heavy cargo and staff across the wider world |
| **8. Enter deeper systems** | Known families combine, then one unfamiliar rule at a time |

## Staff

Hire, pay, and assign company roles. Pawn autonomy and ordinary RimWorld skills and traits are
untouched.

### Certification

Some jobs need a certificate, and you train people into them. A gate operator, a field analyst and
a reserve technician each have a **train** bill:

| Certificate | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Gate operator** | Intellectual 4 |
| **Field analyst** | Intellectual 3 |
| **Reserve technician** | Crafting 5 |

The first two run on a machining table or a crafting spot; the third needs the table. Training is
work over time, and it completes by making that person certified.

A branch with no certified gate operator cannot open a connection. The remedy is a bill, not a
recruitment drive.

### Field history and reporting in

Field history is kept per person. The crew planner shows who is a novice, who is a veteran, and who
has walked the address you are dialling.

Anybody back from a crossing is not dispatched again until debriefed. Quarantine and debrief are
one thing: you do not go back out until you have reported in.

Walking a single colonist through a door by hand is not a company dispatch and is not held to it.

## The record book

**The record book is the company's journal, and it is the thing you actually get paid for.** A crew
carries it through a gate, the survey writes itself as they walk, and the filled book becomes the
case record your researcher analyses.

The company's own books say **company record book** on them. An ordinary textbook bought from a
trader is just a textbook, and the mod leaves it alone.

| Step | What a colonist does |
|---|---|
| **1. Carry it out** | Right-click the book with a colonist and send it through the gate |
| **2. Walk the rooms** | The survey records itself as the crew moves through each required room |
| **3. File it** | Bring it home and store it on the shelf you designated as the records archive |
| **4. Analyse it** | Assign a researcher at a powered bench |

**Right-click a company book and pick *What is this journal for?*** to read those steps in the game,
on the book itself. The same text is on the book's information card.

**You get books four ways.** Each one is a company book and each is labelled as such:

| Where it comes from | When |
|---|---|
| Your scenario | The opening facility places them |
| A company drop | Two arrive when you have a calibrated gate and no book anywhere |
| Procurement | The catalogue sells them |
| A coordinate | Somebody left one down there. It is not yours yet — carry it home and it opens a case |

**Losing a book loses that run's record, not the branch's work.** The company sends more, and it
keeps sending them whenever you have none at all.

## Research

Insight from analysed records buys unlocks across **nine branches**:

| Branch | |
|---|---|
| **Gate engineering and stability** | The window ladder, telemetry, standing connections |
| **Facilities and power** | Reserve capacity, protected rooms, repair practice |
| **Fieldcraft and medicine** | Kits, triage, recovery, training, rescue teams |
| **Measurement and evidence** | Sensors, comparison, confidence, client reports |
| **Spatial mapping and topology** | Atlases, alternate exits, deeper surveys |
| **Entities and containment** | Detection, quarantine, scoped study |
| **Communications and logistics** | Link range, delivery planning, caches, relays |
| **Commerce and organisation** | Better offers, specialist recruitment, leases |
| **Transport and orbital support** | Expansion content only, and never required |

Branches run through **five progression bands**:

| Band | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **0 — Facility foundations** | Make the company safe enough to work |
| **1 — First entry** | Prepare a measured short expedition and read a first return |
| **2 — Repeatable operations** | Revisit known coordinates; reduce preventable failures |
| **3 — Remote operations** | Support more than one site; work beyond headquarters |
| **4 — Deep operations** | Combine known techniques; extend reach |

**A band is a progression band, not a single chain.** Every band above the first is reachable by
more than one path, so a branch is never locked behind one project you have not happened to unlock.

A project's prerequisites are only ever completed logs, a named project below it, and the insight
price. **Nothing requires time to have passed.**

Every unlock grants a capability that real behaviour honours.

## Facilities, equipment and sites

| | |
|---|---|
| **Facility** | A contiguous run of space the branch treats as one place |
| **Equipment link** | Wires a shelf, analyser or cabinet into a gate — across distance and through walls, made by hand |
| **Site** | Somewhere beyond headquarters, on the books. Billed daily, needs people present to receive anything, stops being billed while out of reach |

Nothing about a link is automatic.

## Work across a connection

Colonists travel through an open connection to do work on the other side: hauling, construction,
bills, research, medical, cooking, cleaning, mining, hunting, farming, animals.

- **Work you started keeps going.** A pawn who crossed to finish a job finishes it.
- **Work you have not started waits.** A far-side job sits below every local job of the same type,
  so nobody walks through a gate while the same work exists at home.

The Activity and Connected Work panes report what crossed, and why.

---

## Places held open, and giving one back

Every place you hold open costs a loaded map, as a colony does. The **Places** pane shows the count
against your allowance and lets you hand one back.

**Releasing a place is permanent**, and the confirmation says so: the gate will not open again.

| Before you release | |
|---|---|
| **Anything left there is lost** | The pane counts the items and tells you how many |
| **Not with people inside** | Refused while anybody is there, or part-way through crossing |
| **Not your headquarters** | Refused outright |

A place you found rather than built is a gentler case. Releasing one moves it to a second list, and
selecting the door that led there offers it back.

## When a crew does not come home

A return window can expire with people still on the far side. They are not dead and they are not
removed.

The crew and their cargo stay at the saved address. There are two ways to recover them:

1. **Restore the gate and reopen that route.** Fix whatever failed — power, calibration, the
   operator — and dial the same address.
2. **Send a relief trip.** One extra person per run, and the same relief member is reused for
   further attempts. The original crew is unchanged.

A closed gate does not take your people with it.

## Containment

Holding platforms and what they can hold are the base game's.

What this adds is warnings about the maps you are not looking at — RimWorld's own containment
alerts read the map on screen, and here several maps are live at once.

**On a breach the branch cuts every open connection.** Armed by default, using each gate's own
emergency cutoff.

---

## A branch never dies

Once a branch is in contact with the corporation, it is not written off.

If everyone at the laboratory goes **down** — not dead, down — the company arrives.

| | |
|---|---|
| The site is cleared, and nobody who was there is left standing | |
| The dead are buried on the property, or burned where no ground will take a grave | |
| Damage is repaired, every connection is shut at the gate | |
| Three replacement staff land with supplies | |
| Every bond anywhere on your maps is taken | |
| Up to 25,000,000 is drawn from the account — whatever is there, never below zero | |

You never lose the game. The gate is left commissioned, so the new crew can bring it back up.

The letter gives you the figures. There is no limit on how many times this happens and no
escalating penalty: an investor protecting an investment does not withdraw it for slowness.

> The other two openings keep a gentler version, and **only once the branch has earned contact**: a
> clean-up team that arrives after everyone is actually dead, and leaves five.
