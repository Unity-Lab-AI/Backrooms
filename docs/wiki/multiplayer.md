# Multiplayer

**This mod contains no multiplayer of its own.** No client, no server, no networking of any kind.

The mod that does multiplayer in RimWorld is **[RimWorld Together](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3005289691)**. If you want to play this with somebody, that is the thing you install.

---

## What RimWorld Together does

Each player runs **their own colony, on their own map**. It is not one base with several people in it.

What it links instead is the stories: by its own documentation, **offline visits and raids, and item and pawn exchange** between those separate colonies.

That model is why this mod fits it at all. A branch office is already a thing you run on your own — your facility, your gate, your coordinates, your research, your ledger. Two players are two companies, not one company with two managers.

> **It has its own requirements.** RimWorld Together needs **Harmony**. That is its requirement and not ours — this mod uses no Harmony and needs none.

---

## What it is not

| | |
|---|---|
| **Not a shared colony** | neither this mod nor RimWorld Together provides one |
| **Not a shared map** | there is no shared map in either |
| **Not synchronised research** | research is not synchronised; each branch researches on its own |

Those are not limits we added. **Separate colonies are what RimWorld Together is**, and it says so itself.

---

## What has actually been tested

**Nothing.** No session has been run, by anybody, with these two together.

That is a statement about what has been *verified*, not a prediction. Specifically untested: separate starts, an offline visit, a supply or aid exchange, reconnecting after a drop, and whether anything company-specific travels between players at all.

**Assume nothing company-specific transfers.** Keep each company's facility, gate, discoveries, research and ledger on its own branch, which is where they live anyway.

---

## If you try it

**Everyone needs the same mod list.** RimWorld Together's server does not enforce mod order or settings, so matching them is on you and the people you play with.

Back up your save first. Treat single-player as the supported experience until somebody has run a real session and written down what happened.

Report what you find — see [Links](links.md). A real result is worth more here than anything on this page.
