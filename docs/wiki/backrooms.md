# Beyond the gate

A **coordinate** is an address, and the map it opens onto.

**You visit one. You do not settle one** — the game will say so if you try to put one on the books.

---

## Depth

The shallow rooms are the yellow ones: sparse, and wrong in the way you expect.

Further in it stops pretending. Rooms are furnished as laboratories, workshops, nurseries and
dormitories. Runs of rooms are furnished as one place rather than three. Corridors have benches in
them, because down there that is normal.

## Things move

Go back to a space you have been before and something will not be where you left it.

Nothing tells you. **Not every time.**

---

## Bands

Every coordinate has a **band** — how hostile it currently is.

It rises with **depth** and with **what your colony is worth**, and it decides what comes looking
for your crew.

The Atlas pane prints it. You never have to learn a band by being hurt by it.

## The Atlas

---

## What lives there

Wanderers, the missing, the dead, survivors worth bringing home — and things that are none of
those.

Nothing starves or rots before you have found it. **Discovery starts their clock**, not your
exploring speed.

## What follows you

At the hostile band, an inhabitant will hunt your crew **as far as the threshold**.

Only that far — with one bounded exception: at the deepest band, through an advanced gate, while an
opening is live, something that fits may step through behind them. Then it behaves like anything
else that got inside your base.

**Closing the connection before it arrives is what stops it.**

A gate is never an objective, a lure or a spawn target. Nothing is ever drawn toward one.

## Nothing leaks in

Inhabitants come from this mod's own roster only. **No content from any other mod appears in
Backrooms generation.**

---

## Bringing something back

The point of a crossing is a record.

| Step | Where |
|---|---|
| 1. Record a route, a distortion and an entity observation | In the field |
| 2. Analyse it | A bound laboratory, by a pawn, over time |
| 3. Have a second person review the report | Operations → Investigation |
| 4. Spend the insight | A company project |

The Investigation pane shows which of the three parts each recording still lacks.

## Odd goods

Anything carried out of a coordinate is marked **odd** and never stacks with the ordinary kind.

A thousand odd cotton is a thing you went and fetched, not a thing you grew. Buyers turn up wanting
goods by origin and paying well over the ordinary rate.
