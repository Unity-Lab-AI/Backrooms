# Credits

## This mod

**Rimrooms — Async Industries.** Original source is supplied under [MIT](../../LICENSE).

## RimWorld

RimWorld and its game assemblies are supplied by **Ludeon Studios** and their respective owners.
They are local requirements and are **not bundled** here.

This is a mod for RimWorld. No endorsement is implied.

## Inspiration

**Kane Pixels'** series and the separate **A24** feature inform an indirect story and style
adaptation.

No film frames, footage, audio or dialogue are included. Those rights are separate from this
project's source licence.

## Other mods

Other mods are **separately installed**. None of their files are bundled or modified.

Requiring a mod does not mean its authors support or endorse this one.

## Art and audio

**Everything shipped here is original to this project.** The main-menu backgrounds, the gameplay
textures and the four company sound cues.

**No other package's assets are copied, ever.** Where Rimrooms uses the game's own art or sounds, it
names the path and the game provides it at runtime — nothing is pulled into this package.

Earlier builds shipped no gameplay art at all and this page said so. That changed on 2026-10-06.

## Tooling

Built with Microsoft's .NET SDK. Source inspection used [ILSpy](https://github.com/icsharpcode/ILSpy).

Neither is distributed with the mod.
