## How to use this roster

- **First slice** means the small Async Industries opening already described by the [first playable contract](FIRST_PLAYABLE_CONTRACT.md), [first-slice inventory](FIRST_SLICE_CONTENT_INVENTORY.md), [scenario card](SCENARIOS.md), and [starter threat sheets](THREAT_DESIGN_SHEETS.md). Those documents remain authoritative for its opening details.
- **Designed after the slice** means the scenario or feature has a pre-code concept, but is not part of the first playable build.
- **Later candidate** means an authored content direction that may be selected for a later release. It is not a promise that every listed variation ships.
- Provisional names here are working names. They do not establish canon, final localization, a source-specific creature, or an implementation ID. Stable feature IDs in [FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md](FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md) remain the feature-level traceability keys.
- Use finite, saved destinations and authored mission patterns combined with bounded variation. A generated result must have a readable objective, discoverable tell, recoverable return plan, and persistent case record; “procedural” does not mean endless or arbitrary.

## Room-family and template roster

The families below follow the [procedural space contract](PROCEDURAL_SPACE_CONTRACT.md) and [visual/audio style brief](research/VISUAL_AUDIO_STYLE_BRIEF.md). A destination uses a small compatible subset. Furniture, fixtures, labels, lights, and equipment can change the room's apparent purpose and available evidence; each template still needs valid passages, safe placement, a known exit, and original or properly sourced assets. At the coordinate level, preserve the branch-owned seed, generator/room-library version, room graph, fog-of-war and prior map changes; revisiting restores the saved map instead of generating a replacement. New signals create new coordinate IDs. Equipment may improve what the crew sees or which compatible room/modifier families are selected—for example, scanners expose tags, mapping boards retain route knowledge, repeaters improve signal coverage, and specialized instruments may bias the generator toward relevant features—but equipment never grants magical control over a generated site. See [MOD_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md](MOD_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md#3-full-player-journey-and-operating-cycle) and the authoritative [procedural space contract](PROCEDURAL_SPACE_CONTRACT.md).

## Outpost, gate-duration, and space progression

An outpost is a costly remote responsibility with stock, people, communications, security, and exit conditions. Its physical structures remain on their local map; its branch record tracks the company's contract, staffing intent, costs, and resupply history. The roster follows [RR-OUT](FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md#feature-matrix) and the [economy rules](CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_PROGRESSION.md).

| Progression step | Candidate capability | Safeguard / state visible to the player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HQ only** | Operate the first gate and return crew to the facility. | Operator remains at HQ; crew, kit, power, and recall plan are checked before dispatch.
| **Relay and cache** | Extend a known route with radio contact or a limited supply cache. | Signal quality, stock owner, depletion, local map, and last contact are recorded.
| **Field shelter** | Provide a recoverable rest/resupply point with a named evacuation lead. | Staff, food, medical stock, defense, route, and relief date are visible; shelter is not free map ownership.
| **Guarded station / temporary lab** | Add survey work, secure evidence handling, or client service at a remote site. | Staffing and operating cost are quoted; custody, access, safety, and shutdown are explicit.
| **Leased or monitored space** | Maintain a client-authorized facility, relay, or protected access point. | Lease boundaries, permissions, security, rent, term, renewal, and abandonment effects are shown before signing.
| **Network of sites** | Coordinate shipping, relief crews, rescue, and evacuation across several branch-owned outposts. | Each destination retains its local stock, map, and history; failed links create recovery missions rather than deleting the site.
| **Ground transport** | Carry equipment, provisions, staff, and salvage between surface operations. | Separate purchase, maintenance, crew, cargo, and operating costs; Core campaign remains playable without a vehicle mod.
| **Optional orbital / gravship layer** | Odyssey provides the optional base-game gravship route. VGE Chapter 1 is a candidate native travel/cargo/survival logistics layer; Chapter 2 is a candidate native orbital-threat/defense/salvage layer when its Chapter 1/Odyssey/VEF dependencies and normal access path are present. | Publisher descriptions are not API or compatibility evidence. Preserve native ship, crew, research, finance and operations; Rimrooms may show a tested mission/logistics link only. Do not promise custom boarding missions, stations, moon surfaces, or broader orbital campaigns from these two chapters. All of this is optional and not a gate replacement. See [Gravship Expanded scope](MOD_INTEGRATION_PLAN.md#4-gravship-chapters-and-dependency-policy), [VGE feasibility](research/RWT_AND_GRAVSHIP_FEASIBILITY.md#gravship-chapters-in-the-exact-profile), and [RR-SPACEFLIGHT](FEATURE_TRACEABILITY.md#feature-matrix).
| **Deep-site access** | Combine known room families, route systems, and bounded event rules into finite, persistent destinations. | Saved coordinate identity, finite map, seeded contents, reachable objectives, multiple recovery choices for major sites, and no silent reroll on revisit.

**Superseded 2026-09-28:** the duration model is now the laboratory ladder — 108,000 ticks for a first opening (about thirty real minutes at normal speed), multiplying by three per earned tier, with no countdown at the indefinite tier while power, operator and energy hold. The earlier sketch of 20 minutes, then 2 hours, 1 day, 7 days and 30 days is no longer the schedule. The operating-requirement reasoning in the rest of this paragraph still stands and is what makes the indefinite tier honest. Every extension must explain the related staffing, food, maintenance, power reserve, communications, cargo, and recovery plan; a longer window never creates income by itself. For a goal of weeks or months of Backrooms operations, the design path is several finite missions plus a maintained route/outpost network and relief crews—not one pawn squad held in a single live map for a month. Duration, map residency, and RWT timing need measured limits before this becomes more than a progression ambition.

## Corporation as a physical RimWorld colony

The company is a real RimWorld settlement to operate, not an abstract management screen. Pawn needs, work priorities, skills, passions, traits, beliefs, relationships, health, carrying capacity, construction, power, cooking, storage, trade and ordinary combat remain active. The company layer adds roles, contracts, expedition readiness, gate tasks, evidence custody and the branch ledger; Operations is a shortcut and status view into the pawn, building, map, project, item or contract that actually owns each action. Keep vanilla Work, Architect, Assign, Research, World and pawn controls available, and grow the Company Command layout in stages ([GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md), [SYSTEMS_CATALOG.md](SYSTEMS_CATALOG.md), [OPERATIONS_ACTION_CONTRACTS.md](OPERATIONS_ACTION_CONTRACTS.md)).

## Economy, purchasing and physical supply flow

The company economy is a branch-local USD Company Account, with one account unit equal to one dollar, within the confirmed multi-trillion-dollar parent-company setting scale. Company receipts and invoices never become silver stacks. RimWorld silver, steel, components, food, furnishings, weapons, tools, samples and research dossiers remain physical goods with stockpiles, hauling, mass, storage and delivery limits. The opening allocation, contract payouts, payroll and other exact figures in the linked [economy model](CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_MODEL.md), [economy progression](CAMPAIGN_ECONOMY_PROGRESSION.md), and [campaign state dictionary](CAMPAIGN_STATE_DICTIONARY.md) remain editable balance targets for modeling and playtests, not owner-fixed amounts.

The player earns from accepted surveys, rescue/recovery, evidence delivery, services, leases and eligible item sales. Before commitment, a contract/order shows scope, USD amount, advance, bonus, penalty cap, ownership, shipment quantity, receiving site, arrival window, the several routes to success, and consequence of failure. **There is no deadline**; an arrival window is when goods are expected, not a limit on the player. Paying a company order posts once and creates an order; the physical delivery creates items only on arrival. Materials can also be grown, crafted, mined, hauled or traded through ordinary RimWorld systems. Contracts, payroll, rent, staffing, repairs, food/medicine supply and outpost service are separate visible transactions. Every missing, delayed or lost shipment retains a receipt and recovery case. RWT can transfer physical goods only through a tested route; company cash and local research do not follow the shipment.
