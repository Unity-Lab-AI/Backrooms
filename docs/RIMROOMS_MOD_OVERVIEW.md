### Build a company. Open the machine. Discover what lies beyond.

*A RimWorld 1.6 company-management campaign: build a research facility, open an interdimensional gate, and bring your people home.*

## Run the facility

Begin with a few staff, limited supplies, and an unfinished gate. Hire researchers, engineers, guards, and field crews; build labs, power, storage, quarters, food service, recreation, and defenses. RimWorld’s pawn jobs handle growing, cooking, crafting, research, and hauling physical stock.

Plan a crew, gear, and destination, then survey, retrieve, rescue, investigate, or withdraw. Research extends gate openings and improves return options. Reports, samples, witnesses, missing crews, recoveries, and strange objects become cases, contracts, and research leads.

## Explore a changing world

Each seeded coordinate persists for mapping and return: repeating corridors, altered rooms, abandoned supplies, impossible connections, and mysteries learned through evidence. Fog, route clues, radio, and return plans guide crews home. Secured spaces become labs, shelters, depots, or outposts. Town openings bring investigations and rescue decisions.

All three starts converge on shared exploration, evidence, research, threats, and company systems. Later progression adds outposts, ground transport, and optional Odyssey/Vanilla Gravship Expanded space operations; the gate remains the main route into the Backrooms.

## Think like a corporation

The parent company operates at multi-trillion-dollar scale. Discovery and contract values depend on the buyer, evidence, and deliverables; events do not pay a flat fee. USD stays in each branch’s ledger. Goods remain physical and subject to carrying, storage, hauling, and shipment limits that players expand through staffing, facilities, warehouses, and transport.

## Cooperate and make it yours

With RimWorld Together, players exchange resources, items, technology, and facility visits across separate branches, subject to testing on the chosen build. No live map control: finances, maps, discoveries, and gate state stay local.

Company Command remaps navigation around staff, facilities, expeditions, cases, research, and finance. Original rotating menu art previews playable content with readable controls and motion settings. Kane Pixels and A24 inspire the atmosphere; gameplay and campaign content are original.
